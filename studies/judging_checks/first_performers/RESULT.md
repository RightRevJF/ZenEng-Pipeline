## What it says

- Counting only other artists' versions changes nothing by itself (−0.041 against −0.042). R04-2's first worry, that an artist's own re-releases were inflating the counts, is answered: they were not.
- Who performed the song first changes a good deal. Where the artist wrote or first sang the song, the lean is −0.026. Where the artist was already covering it, −0.10: four times as strong, on a quarter of the songs. That second figure is under the power floor and is an estimate, but it is the larger half of where 749.157 came from.
- A plain reading: among songs an artist *chose to cover*, the ones with moving chords are the much-covered ones, the standards. That is a fact about which songs become standards and get picked up again, mixed into what was read as a fact about an artist's own songs. For the artist's own songs the direction is the same and the size about six tenths.
