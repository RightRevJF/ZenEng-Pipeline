# The harmony under the lead, third and last form: it rests where nothing fits under the lead (749.222)

27 November 2026. The songwriting session's form, marks and stopping rule, from the thread (`coord/songwriting.md`, about 03:50), set down before any run. Forms 1 and (a) each met five marks of six and missed H3 in song 59.

**The form.** Never over the lead. **Where no note under the lead fits a kept class within the stated range, the harmony rests on that syllable.** The fit is started from the song's present weights, as in (a); the classes are the kept ones; the stated range is the voice next below the lead's, from its floor to the lead voice's top.

**How "no note under the lead fits" is read, fixed here before the run.** The engine already has this test, and it is the one that now sends the harmony over the lead: `room_below`, true where some kept interval under the lead (a third, fourth, fifth, sixth or octave) lands on a tone of the chord inside the range. Where it is false, the present engine may go over the lead; forms 1 and (a) fell back on a unison or on a scale note that is not of the chord. In this form the harmony rests there. The range in the test is the stated range.

**Marks (the songwriting session's).**
- **H1** no note over the lead. **H2** every note in the stated range.
- **H3** the fit to kept interval shares no worse than now by more than 0.03, in every song, unchanged.
- **H4, loosened by design and said so before the run:** no fewer than 0.85 of the syllables sung now, in every song.
- **H5** nothing else in any record changed. **H6** every true band check stays true.
- **Reported:** which syllables rest, by song and section.
- **Stopping rule:** if this misses any mark, 749.222 is closed for the version, the finding on the list. No fourth form.

On the same nine records.
