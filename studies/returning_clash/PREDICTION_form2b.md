**Form 2b.** The melismas are drawn exactly as in v52, and the engine chooses its tune reading them exactly as in v52. Only when the chosen tune's melismas are written out: a second note that would be a clash (a beat long or more, off its chord, a semitone from a chord tone) takes the scale step above where that is clear of the chord, the run cap and the range; where it is not, the syllable keeps its one note. No melisma is moved to another syllable. `LUTHIER_MELISMA_CLEAR=after`; spec key `melisma_clear: "after"`. Off.

By construction the chosen build, its pool, its fits and every note but those second notes are v52's. So the marks are few.

**Marks (the six seen songs written through; and each v52 record's fitted spec, 1,000 builds, read as sung with the form):**
- **B1.** Clashes on a melisma's second note at most a quarter of v52's, per 100 notes, in every song (builds), and none in any of the six chosen tunes.
- **B2.** Each written record is v52's but for its melody's melisma notes and what is derived from them (checks, parts that follow the tune): the same chord plan, the same pick, the same pool and living builds, the same notes on every syllable's first note.
- **B3.** Soundness of the three holds, read as sung.
- **B4.** Melismas in 100 syllables no lower than v52's by more than one point in any song (builds); the share on a line's last syllable within 5 points.
- **B5.** All clashes per 100 notes no more than v52's in any song (builds).

**What I expect.** All five met. B4: about one melisma in fifteen clashes now; perhaps half of those can take the step above, so the share falls by 0.2 to 0.3 of a point. B3: the same three builds with a handful of second notes changed or gone; rhythm scaling as sung should move in the third decimal.
