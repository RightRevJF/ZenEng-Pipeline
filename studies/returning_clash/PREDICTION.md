# Clashes in returning lines: a reading, its rule written first (749.212)

26 November 2026. Seen before writing: `studies/borrow_kept/RESULT_clash.md` (in v50, song 55's lines sung again or copied by the plan carry 1.15 clashes per 100 notes, 0.83 of them long in the draw's own sense; the other five songs 0.28 to 0.51), and the code: a copied line (`how` copy) is its source's notes as they stand, with no clash rule; a line sung again (`again()`) has the clash rule of 833, which moves a long note that clashes to a chord tone within four semitones inside the register and leaves it where there is none. Nothing below has been counted.

**A clash**, as in the marks since the thread's ruling of 12:10: a sung note a beat long or more, off its chord and a semitone from a chord tone, other than the blue notes 846 allows.

**Read** (v50 fitted specs, 600 builds, songs 55, 54, 58, 49, 50, 59), every clash in a line that returns, by:
- **how the line was made:** copied, or sung again;
- **whether its chord differs from the source's:** the chord under the note against the chord under the same place of the source line;
- **for a line sung again:** whether the note was long in the draw's own sense (which is what 833 tests) or only as sung; and whether a chord tone was in reach (within four semitones, in the scale, in the register).

**Rule.** A cause is **named** if it holds at least half of the returning lines' clashes in song 55 and at least half in the other five songs taken together:
- **A. a copied line over another chord** (no rule looks at it);
- **B. a line sung again, long in the draw's sense, no chord tone in reach** (the rule looked and found nothing);
- **C. a line sung again, long only as sung** (the rule tests the wrong length);
- **D. the same chord as the source's** (the clash is the source's own, carried over).

**What follows if named.** A: the clash rule of 833 applied to a copied line where the chord under a long note differs from its source's. B: a second resort, a scale step away from the semitone. C: the rule reads the sung length. D: the fault is upstream, in whatever let the source clash. Marks first: clashes in returning lines no more than half of v50's in every song where v50 has 0.3 per 100 notes or more; and the rest of v50's standing marks (soundness, pools, the words' fit within 0.02 either way being read one-sided as "no lower by more than 0.02", bars on a figure already sung no lower by more than 0.03, since moving notes in a returning line costs repeats).

**What I expect.** A named: song 55's plan changes chords under its returning sections more than the others', and a copy is blind to that. If instead D is the largest, I do not understand where the source's clash comes from, since the draw forbids it on a long note, and the reading will have found something else worth knowing.
