# Plant the peak, second trial: the rule, written before the trial

The first trial's planting took in only 22 to 54 builds in 100, so it tested a way of drawing more than the idea. This trial places the peak two surer ways, on songs 55, 54 and 58 and, read only, on the hold-out song 59.

- A60: as form A before (the planted line redrawn in a raised register until its highest stressed note is the peak), with up to 60 tries.
- C: the planted line is drawn once in the raised register and then moved whole, by scale steps, until its highest stressed note is the peak (the peak placed directly, the line's shape kept).

**The check is the same** (the session's): on 100 tries a song, the builds' span within 2 semitones of the aim in at least half the builds, and the share of notes on the top note inside the kept 10th to 90th in 9 builds in 10. A form passes if it meets both in songs 55, 54 and 58. Song 59 is read beside them and decides nothing.

Also read: the share of builds in which the peak was planted. Under 80 in 100 means the form is still not a fair test.
