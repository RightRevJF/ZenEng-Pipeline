# Plant the peak, one thing changed: the rule for a fourth trial, written before it is run

**Seen before writing:** RESULT3.md. The third trial changed two things at once (the peak planted in the first fresh chorus line; the verse's and bridge's tops lowered under it) and failed mostly on builds surviving. It did not read which gate the builds died at.

**This trial changes one thing.** The peak is planted as before (first fresh chorus line, highest stressed note at the floor plus the range the words ask, redrawn up to 60 times, or drawn once and moved whole). Every part's top and centre stay as the engine sets them now.

**Also read:** for every form, which gate or build fault each dead try dies at. The third trial's form (tops lowered) is run again beside the others only so its deaths can be read.

**The check** is the third trial's, unchanged: a form passes if, in at least three of songs 55, 54, 58 and 60, (1) the builds span within 2 semitones of the asking in at least half the builds; (2) the share of notes on the top note is inside the kept 10th to 90th in at least 8 builds in 10; (3) at least 7 in 10 as many builds pass the gates as now; (4) the words' fit falls by no more than 0.02. Song 59 is read and decides nothing.

**What I expect:** builds survive (check 3 met) because the tops are no longer moved; the range is reached less often than in the third trial because the bridge and verse may still sit where they did. If check 1 fails and check 3 passes, the lowering of the tops was what cost the builds, and the next question is the range alone.

If both forms pass, the one with more builds inside the range is built. If neither, nothing is built.
