# Plant the peak as a note: the rule for a fifth trial, written before it is run

**Seen before writing:** RESULT4.md: planted builds die at gate 838 because the planted line is drawn in a raised register and lifts the chorus's median.

**The change.** The first fresh chorus line keeps the centre the engine gives it. Only its top is set to the peak (floor plus the range the words ask), and it is redrawn, up to 60 times, until its highest stressed note is the peak. Nothing else changes; every part's top stays.

**The check** is the third trial's, unchanged (three songs of 55, 54, 58, 60: range within 2 of the asking in half the builds; top-note share inside the kept 10th to 90th in 8 builds in 10; 7 in 10 as many builds survive; words' fit down by no more than 0.02). Song 59 read only. Deaths read by gate.

**What I expect:** builds survive in at least three songs; the range is reached about as often as in the fourth trial; the peak planted less often than before (the line must climb from its own centre), most likely again short in song 54.
