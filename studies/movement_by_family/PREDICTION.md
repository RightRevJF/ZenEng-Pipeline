# Does movement differ by genre family in kept songs? The rule, written before the data is read (749.129)

**Why.** Under 944 every song moves at the kept middle, so every song moves alike. The writer's ruling allows a style guide. The songwriting session asks: take the default movement from the kept songs of the song's own genre family, its middle and not one song's, if the families differ.

**Seen before writing:** Table HF of Window 196 (the four songs' builds all within a quarter width of the kept middle). No kept song's movement was read by family.

**Data.** The 160 kept 4/4 songs, in the three families the engine already uses for its scale degrees (studies/genre: soul and R&B, country, all others; a hand tagging by artist).

**Read:** moves by step, moves by leap, repeated notes.

**The rule.** A reading **differs by family** if (a) shuffling the family labels 2,000 times gives a spread between the three family middles as large as the true one fewer than 5 times in 100, and (b) the true spread between the highest and lowest family middle is at least a quarter of the kept middle half's width for that reading.

- If two or three of the readings differ, the default movement is taken from the song's family.
- If one differs, that reading alone is taken from the family.
- If none differs, the kept middle stays for every song, and the paper says the families move alike.

A family with fewer than 15 kept songs is not used (its songs keep the kept middle).
