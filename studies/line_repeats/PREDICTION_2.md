# Two more readings, their rules written first (for 749.205 step 1, and the one I proposed)

26 November 2026. Seen before writing: `RESULT.md` here (kept repeats are at the same pitch 82 times in 100; neither mark met), and the list for v51's addendum 7, which asks that repeats be **counted apart**: a section's own returns, and a figure borrowed into a new line (what 818 does).

## A. Counted apart (the session's ask)

The kept table has no label for "a return". I split by the words: a repeat whose two lines share at least half their words (in order, by the shorter line) is **a return**; any other is **a borrowed figure**, new words on an old figure, which is what 818 makes. Same repeat measure as before; heights in scale steps, with direction.

**Rule.** "A borrowed figure at another height" is named if at least 25 in 100 of kept verses' borrowed figures (line before or two before) are at a height other than 0, and they number at least 40. Same mark as before, now on the borrowed figures alone.

**What I expect.** Returns nine in ten at the same pitch. Borrowed figures more often moved than returns, perhaps 25 to 30 in 100, so near the mark either way. I do not hold it firmly.

## B. The height of a line against the line two before it

The earlier result: in three songs of six the engine's line sung again is no longer a repeat, yet the ladder says borrowing is what brings the lines to one height. So read the height directly, repeat or not.

**Measure.** For each verse line from a part's third on (four notes or more, and the line two before it too): the difference between the two lines' middle notes, in scale steps. A line is **at the same height** if that is under one step. Read for kept songs (by song, songs with six such lines or more) and for the builds (v50 fitted specs, 300 builds, the same six songs), repeats and other lines apart.

**Rule.** "The engine's lines sit at the height of the line two before" is named if in at least four songs of six the builds' share of **non-repeating** verse lines at the same height is over the kept 75th by song.

**What follows if named.** Not a form yet. It would say the fault is in what "sing it again" keeps when it changes the line, and the next step would be to read which of its parts holds the height.

**What I expect.** Named: built 55 to 70 in 100 at the same height against a kept middle near 35 to 40. If it is not named, then the ladder's drop in spread at the borrow rung comes from something other than the height two lines back, and I do not know what.
