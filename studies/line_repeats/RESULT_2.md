## What it comes to

- Kept verses: about 1 line in 9 repeats a nearby line, at the same pitch; the rest sit at the earlier line's height about a third of the time.
- The engine: the borrow fires 8 times in 10 from a part's third line on. The line it makes is usually changed past being a repeat, but it keeps the earlier line's height. So the engine has the kept sameness of height three lines in four, with few true repeats to show for it (songs 55, 54, 59), or many (58, 49, 50).
- The fault is not where a borrowed line is sung. It is **how often the borrow fires, against how often a kept line repeats**. 0.8 was fitted to the share of bars on a figure already sung (818), a different count.
