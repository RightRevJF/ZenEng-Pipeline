## What is counted
1. **A section's size and vocabulary, like for like.** For each sung section of each living build, and for each pop clip: the bars with a note in them, the number of different bar rhythms, and the share of bars on a rhythm already sung in that section (K6's own measure). Also both read on the first 16 sung bars only, since the clips run 16 bars at the median and a longer section has more chances to repeat.
2. **Which bars are new, by what they hold.** Every sung bar of a section is sorted by the lines whose notes it holds: one line only, or the end of one line with the start of the next; and by how its (first) line was made (fresh, title, the lyric's again, 818's again, a copy), whether that line had its source's syllable count, and whether its slot is two bars or four. For each sort: its share of the bars, and how often it is on a rhythm already sung.
3. **How many different two-bar rhythms a section draws** for its lines drawn fresh, against how many different two-bar units a clip holds.

## What I expect
- The engine's sections hold more different bar rhythms than a pop clip of the same length: I say about twice as many in 16 bars. That is the cause in one line: each line drawn fresh takes a new rhythm from a table of hundreds, where a pop section is built from two or three.
- Bars holding two lines' notes (a line's end and the next line's pickup) are a small share and nearly always new.
- Lines sung again with their source's count sit on rhythms already sung nearly always; lines with a note added or taken make one new bar each.
