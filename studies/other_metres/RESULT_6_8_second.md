## Why, by arithmetic and not by guess
749.232 gives the line ahead of a title line one bar more and makes it begin its last syllable on or before the first beat of its last bar. On song 52 those lines were thirteen syllables and went from three bars to four: two two-bar windows, and the rule could be kept. Here the line has six syllables and goes from two bars to **three**. A three-bar phrase is drawn as a two-bar window and a one-bar window, with a third of the syllables (two) in the one-bar window. But the rule lets that last bar hold a syllable on its first beat only. Two syllables cannot both begin there, so no window ever fits. Every build dies on that line.
