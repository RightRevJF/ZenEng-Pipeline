## What it says
- In every stopped build the line ahead was made by singing another line again (a line the lyric marks as sung again, or 818), and its last syllable begins **after its own slot has ended**, inside the next line's bars. The next line is then left a start half a bar or more into its two bars, with fewer places on the grid than it has syllables. No window, kept or generated, can fit that.
- A fresh line may not do this: its last syllable must begin a breath before its slot ends. A line sung again is not held to that limit.
- **So I was wrong at 18:50 ET**, to Jason and here, that every failure was a missing entry in the phrasebook. These failures are a line sung again running over. That is my fifth wrong cause today, and the first I stated to the writer as fact. The grammar (749.239) is still his direction and still worth fitting; it is not what stops these builds, and it would not have fixed them.
