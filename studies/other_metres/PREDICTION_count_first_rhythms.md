# The first-rhythms count: what is counted, before any code or run

28 November 2026, 11:04 ET. Ordered by the songwriting session (10:58 ET). A count on builds we have; no form; seen seeds only.

A correction first, promised in my 10:57 post: "the first rhythms the section happens to draw" cannot be what a seed fixes, since they differ build by build inside a seed. What differs between seeds is drawn once a run. This count therefore has two parts.

## Part 1: within a seed, do the first rhythms decide?
On living builds of the present package (749.259 at 64) for song 55's words (seed 10's record) and song 49's words (seeds 10 and 11's records, which stand at 60.1 and 97.8 on the six-mark share at one lift): each build sorted by
- whether the first line drawn in verse 1 has two like bars (its first two bars the same rhythm), and the same for chorus 1;
- how many different bar rhythms the first verse ended with (and the first chorus).
For each sort: the builds, the share meeting all six like marks, the share meeting K6's rhythm line, and the median of rhythm again within sections.

## Part 2: what differs between two seeds of one lyric
Song 49's words, seeds 10 and 11: every entry of the two records' plans that differs (all that the planner draws or fits once a run), listed.

## What I expect
- Part 1: builds whose first verse line has two like bars meet the six marks more often, by 10 to 25 in 100; builds whose first verse ends with eight or fewer different bar rhythms meet them nearly always, those with eleven or more seldom. The first rhythms matter within a seed.
- Part 2: the two seeds differ in the registers, the key, the fitted move weights and the title's seed-drawn things; I do not know which bears on rhythm. I expect at least one difference that touches the bars: the bars of sung phrases (749.226's plan) or the shared bars.
