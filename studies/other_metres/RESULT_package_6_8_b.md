With the first five eighths barred, no kept window (they begin on or near the bar line) fits, and the line is set by the weighted rule in what is left: most often onsets at 30, 36, 54, 72, 78, 84, 90, crowded into two and a half bars of its four, the last bar and a half unused.
- So the title line had the bars and could not use them: **its room is taken at the front by the line before it**, and the count of windows, which assumes the line may start where its slot starts, promised what the draw could not do.

The padded form this morning left a bar's rest before such a line; the folk form removes it; and my title's-room rule looked only inside the title line.
