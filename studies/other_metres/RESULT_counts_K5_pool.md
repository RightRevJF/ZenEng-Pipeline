## Plainly
- **K5 at seed 3 is old, not ours.** The package as it stood before tonight has the same fault at seed 3: its pool's median commonest pitch is 0.31 and 46.2 in 100 of the pool meet K5 (tonight's: 0.318 and 41.0).
- **What the seed fixes, and what differs at seed 3:** the plan's lift (the chorus's centre over the verse's, 838) and the registers are drawn once a run, and they are the same in both packages at each seed (checked). The lift is 4, 6 and 1 at seeds 1, 2 and 3. At seed 3 verse and chorus sit on almost one centre (68 and 69). K5 goes with the lift at all three seeds in both packages: lift 1, 41.0 in 100 meet it; lift 4, 72.3; lift 6, 98.0. Three seeds is a pattern, not a proof.
- **It is not a narrow register in the sense asked.** The registers at seed 3 are the widest of the three; the span the whole song may use is much the same. What is narrow is the distance between verse and chorus, so the two parts sing the same pitches. (The pick's 12 semitones at seed 3 is one build; the pool's median range is 15.0.)
- **How 4/4 picks:** the same way. "past both gates: the old charge's least half, then the joint gate; the pick is the first of them in the order built"; then the first at or over the range floor, then the first inside the voice. 4/4's pool is cut by more gates than 3/4's (song 54: 140 past the old charge, 140 past the joint gate, 36 past the words' gate, 36 past the page floor, 22 in the pool); the 3/4 pool is cut only by the first two.
- **4/4's pool on its own six marks: 63.6 to 81.8 in 100 (median 76.2), five songs.** Pop 3/4's pool on the same six: 76.9 to 91.1. On like marks 3/4 is at or above 4/4's standard at every seed. The first-past-the-gates pick is how the engine works in every metre, and 4/4's pool is not all inside its own bands either.
- **But the six leave out exactly where 3/4 misses.** On all nine, 3/4's pool is 36.5, 40.1 and 23.9. K3 and K5 have no by-song mark in the 4/4 table; 4/4 holds them as gates (731; 956's ceiling), so its pool meets them by construction. Held to "at least 4/4's share on like marks", 3/4 passes at all three seeds; whether that is the right bar when the unlike marks are the weak ones is yours to say.
- A correction to my rule file: I called 54, 55 and 52 the 4/4 byte-check songs. Song 52's record is 6/8. The five 4/4 songs read are 54, 55, 59, 61 and 66 (the engine's last 4/4 records in the repository); each run gave its record's own pick.
