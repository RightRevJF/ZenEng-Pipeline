## "Free", in the songwriting session's words
"A metre's median inside the 4/4 table's middle half, and its 90th inside the 4/4 90th's bootstrap range" (the 4/4 tunes of the same source resampled 2,000 times, seed 0, the 5th to 95th of the 90th). One adaptation, said now: where a gate acts on the low side (plainness, a floor at the 10th), the 10th is read the same way and is the side that decides; both tails are listed. For 959 the share of tunes holding the leap is read against the 4/4 share's bootstrap range.

A check is **metre-free** only if free in reference 1 and in reference 2. Where they disagree, "the pop side decides for pop songs, and the finding is a genre gap, not a metre one." Counts of tunes beside every figure.
