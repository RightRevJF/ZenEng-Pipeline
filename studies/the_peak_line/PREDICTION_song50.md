The sixth asked that the peak be planted as often as in v47, and I have since found that v47's count was made by the fault the form corrects (749.193). I cannot strike a mark after the fact. So the marks below are written now, and they are read on a song that neither I nor any form has run.

**Song 50** (`songs/p60/lyric50.json`; folk, baritone, 88). Run once by the engine as it is (v47's notes), once with the form, a thousand builds each; and its v47 fitted spec run again with only the form changed.

1. Living builds above v47's (the whole engine).
2. The builds' middle words' fit within 0.02 of v47's.
3. The range is kept: the form's chosen tune at or over its floor; and on the fitted spec, gate on, the builds' middle range no more than a semitone under v47's.

All four, and the form is built, on by default, as fix 964. Any miss, by any margin, and it is not built and stays behind its switch. Either way song 50 is spent, and becomes a seen song.

Reported and deciding nothing: the peak planted against v47's; the octave gate's count; the chosen tunes' ranges and words' fit; whether song 50's v47 tune is sound (it has never been built).

And song 50 is a baritone folk song at 88, slower and lower than most of the eight; if the form fails anywhere it could be here.
