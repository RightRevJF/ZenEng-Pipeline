## What it says (749.193)

More draws plant the peak more often than v47 ever did, and bring the leaps back with them. The reason is the same one that undid the three forms of 749.189, seen now with the odds right: **the plant works by drawing a line again until it happens to touch a top that has been set far above it, and the likeliest way for a line to touch a far top is to leap to it.** Every extra draw is another chance for the leap. Drawing until an outcome arrives selects for the outcome's easiest road (749.181 was the same thing in the chord writer).

So the number of draws is not a setting to tune. It is the size of a side effect. At 60 with the odds right, the side effect is about the size kept songs show.

## Where this leaves 749.192

Two things are now separate that I had tied together:

1. **The shared bin is a correction.** The draw gave every pitch past an octave the weight kept songs give to all of them together. With it shared, at the plant's own 60 draws, five marks of six held on eight songs, and the leaps sit at the kept rate with no gate.
2. **Mark 6 was the wrong mark.** It asked that the peak be planted as often as in v47. But v47's count was itself made by the error: the plant reached its top so often because leaps past an octave were offered at several times their odds. I set v47's count as the standard before I knew that. A mark that can only be met by restoring the fault is not measuring the mend.

I cannot change a mark after the fact and call the test passed. What can be done honestly is to say which mark I would set now, before a song that has seen none of this is run:
