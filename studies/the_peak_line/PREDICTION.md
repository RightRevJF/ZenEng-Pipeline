# The line that carries the top: a reading, its rule written first (749.189)

25 November 2026. Seen before writing: the three forms of the mend and their results (`studies/the_draw`). Nothing of this reading has been run. It changes nothing in the engine.

**Question.** Fix 950 gives one chorus line the song's top by raising that line's top and leaving its centre. Three forms of choosing among that line's draws could not give both the peak and few leaps. Is the line's centre the fault: do kept songs lift the whole line that carries their top, where the engine lifts only its ceiling?

**Kept songs** (`kept_corpus.kept_songs()`). In each, the line that first holds the song's highest note (lines of three notes or more):
- **the reach:** the top less the line's middle pitch, in semitones;
- **the lift:** the line's middle pitch less the middle pitch of the other lines of the same part (verse, chorus, bridge) in that song;
- **the approach:** the interval into the top note (a step, to 2; a skip, 3 to 7; a leap, 8 to 12; past an octave), and whether the top is the line's first note;
- the part the line is in.

**The builds.** Songs 55, 54, 58 and 49: each v47 record's fitted spec, a thousand builds, the octave gate off, living builds in which the peak was planted. The same four measures for the line that first holds the build's top. And from the spec, the plant's own geometry: the peak less the carrying line's centre as the registers set it.

**Rule.** The centre is named the fault if, in at least three songs of the four, both hold:
1. the builds' middle reach is over the kept 75th of reach;
2. the builds' middle lift is under the kept middle lift by 2 semitones or more.

If it is named, the change it points to: the carrying line's centre is raised so that the peak sits the kept middle reach above it (never lower than the line's own centre, never past the part's top), and the line is then drawn as 950 draws it. Its marks will be written after this reading and before any run.

If only one holds, or neither, the reading is reported and no change is tried on it.

**What I expect.** Kept: a reach of about 4 to 6 semitones and a lift of 2 to 4 (the line that carries the top sits high as a whole). Builds: a reach of 8 or more and a lift near 0. So both parts of the rule to hold. If kept songs turn out to reach their top from a low line as the engine does (a reach of 8 or more), then the leaps come from somewhere else in how the line is drawn and I am wrong about the centre.
