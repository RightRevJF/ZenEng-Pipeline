Living builds rise in all eight songs, by 9 to 55 in 100. The words' fit does not move. Every chosen tune is at or over its floor. The first three songs stay sound and their pools are sounder. That is every mark the session set for 749.189, and all of mine but one.

Mark 6 fails because the plant had been leaning on the fault. With every pitch past an octave offered at the octave's weight, a line whose top had been raised reached that top easily, by leaping.
