# realness_covers: result (counted; K = 4, α .0125)

**Predicted (rule 6821d6b):** within an artist, a song that looks least like an engine build (cross-fitted realness) is taken up a little more: +0.02.

**Came out:** **−0.023** (SE 0.011, p .038; range −0.045 to −0.001), 8,565 songs, 1,617 artists; halves −0.007 and −0.038; conditional logit −0.13. With pitch-class entropy also held: −0.019 (p .09). **Killed** (+0.02 lies outside the range), and the sign is the other way.

**What it means.** "Sounds like a real pop song" (more pitch variety, wider range, longer phrases) is not "gets covered": within an artist, the songs that are more like the typical hit are, if anything, taken up slightly less, in line with run 11's finding that fewer pitch classes go with uptake. So the gap list is a craft list (how to make the engine sound real), not a greatness list. One variable sits on both lists: rhythm repetition (see formula_search).

**What it cannot say.** Seen songs. The realness score mixes many features; it was trained to tell real from engine, not for covers. Credit: Hooktheory via Sheet Sage (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0); Discogs-VI 20240701 (CC BY-SA 4.0).
