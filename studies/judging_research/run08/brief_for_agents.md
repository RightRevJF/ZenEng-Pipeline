# Run 08 brief for the agents (27 November 2026, the 02:17 ET pass)

Project: Jason Bryant's search for a way to judge great music from the notes alone (pitch, rhythm, harmony, form; words set aside). Yardstick: lasting love plus covers by other artists; not critics, not popularity alone. Jason's ideas: music as shapes (a beautiful statue versus a humdrum one), Hilbert space, non-Euclidean geometry, his S = 1 + g/pi, the vector of expectation, surprise and memory. Mathematics focus: geometry first. Method: first principles (question every requirement, delete, simplify, speed the test loop). Every tested idea states beforehand what would kill it. Jason's rule: every idea that can be tested on the data at hand is tested this pass.

## Rulings now in force (new this pass)
- **Power floor (ruled, R06-5, in the data pack README):** a test counts toward the shared bar (.05 / number of counted tests) only if its power is 0.2 or more, reckoned before the outcome is joined (fake outcomes on the real design). Tests under the floor are still frozen and committed, and reported as estimates: they pass nothing and kill nothing.
- **Range kill (R07-3, agreed by the maintainer, Jason may overrule):** a counted test is killed only when its written prediction lies outside the estimate's own 95% range; otherwise a non-pass is "unsettled".
- **Power is simulated at the realised base rate / real outcome spread.**
- A pass also needs the predicted sign in both artist (composer) halves, split by a hash of the name, and a second model of the same sign.
