# cover_timeline: result (R08-L2 + R08-G3; card 2)

## In plain words
Under pure copying (card 2), a song with twice the recordings in its first ten years should get twice the new ones in years 10–29 (β = 1). The Listener predicted more, β = 1.10, which would signal a hidden pull.

**β came out at 0.91, below 1.** Doubling early versions brings about 1.88 times the late versions, not 2. Early leaders still lead, but their advantage fades (sublinear attachment). The prediction is killed. Pure copying is rejected too, in the other direction.

**Urn ceiling.** We simulated a pure-luck world: each cohort's real late total is dealt out in proportion to early counts. Luck alone gives about half the observed spread in late covers once early covers are known (0.35 vs 0.67). The other **48%** is the most that anything about a song could explain: its notes, fame, publishers or data errors.

## Numbers
- Power printed first: 0.25 frozen (rerun 0.25), and 0.35 on the real K10.
- Primary model, 1950–94 (3,931 of 4,020 songs; 935 composers): β = 0.907, SE 0.030, 95% range 0.849–0.966, p(β = 1) = .002.
- Halves: 0.905 and 0.915.
- Second model (1950–89): 0.897, range 0.832–0.962.

## Verdict
**KILLED: 1.10 lies outside the 95% range.** It cannot be a pass, because β < 1 everywhere. The kill does not depend on α, so moving K from 5 to 6 changes nothing.

It is firm. 1.10 lies 6.4 SE above the estimate, and 1 lies 3.1 SE above it. In the power runs, the kill fired by mistake only about 8% of the time when β was truly 1.10.

## Renaming check
β itself uses no notes measure. All five gamma measures pass their gates: stability .60–.77, and |ρ| ≤ .29 with both ch_rep and V. Agreement across sources is weaker (.31–.58; ch_rep is lowest at .31).

## What it cannot say
- **Dating noise.** Reissues and missing years make K10 noisy, which pulls β toward 0. Part of the shortfall may be this: the worst-dated band (pre-1950) is lowest.
- **Discogs coverage** grows over time, and the cohort bins absorb that only on average.
- **What the 48% holds.** Fame, publishers and chart writers sit in it alongside the notes.
- **Confounds for the gamma readings:** genre within composer, jazz idiom, name matching, and the selection of tunes that have charts.
- **The notes are not cleared.** Content may act through K10 itself.
