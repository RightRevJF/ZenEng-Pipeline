The searches were aimed at (1) the founding curvature paper, (2) a curvature that is plain linear algebra (resistance curvature), and (3) the known pitfalls of curvature on tiny directed graphs, so that tonight's experiment rules are not naive.

Tier 1 (item 3 lists the 2024 companion; the 2022 paper is the founding construction). Picked because it is plain linear algebra with no free setting, quick on 3,000 songs, and gives a second, independent curvature to set beside Ollivier's.
