## The fitness estimator (frozen and committed before any notes measure is joined: `run12/experiments/fitness/fitness.py`, commit b0c7824)
A Price / Bianconi-Barabási growth model with aging, fitted on the 2,627 matched Discogs-VI groups from cover years alone: covers of song i in year t ~ Poisson(η_i · (k_i(t−) + A) · g(age) · h(era)), η_i ~ Gamma(r, r). FIT_i = posterior mean of log η_i. **Outcome-side facts (no notes read):** the likelihood rises monotonically as A grows (A → 2×10⁶): the cover timing shows **no rich-get-richer attachment** beyond song-to-song differences in rate; r = 0.66 (fitness varies a lot between songs); FIT correlates 0.985 with log(1 + covers) once first year is held. So FIT is close to an age- and era-adjusted, shrunk cover rate. Imaginers do not open FIT values; this summary is all you need. (Aging: g is 1.5 at age 1, then about 0.45 to 0.6 from age 4 on.)
