## THE DATA THIS PASS: Hooktheory melodies, within artist (allowed: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0)
- 9,588 songs by 1,741 artists with two or more songs (run 04's rows), each with 1 to several clips (sections) of melody and chords. Median clip about 47 notes. **These songs are SEEN:** their outcome has been joined to chord measures (runs 04-06; chord repetition within artist about −0.04) and a few melody/rhythm measures. No measure of the melody's pitch graph, pitch-class entropy, or Cheeger structure has been joined to them. The songs are re-spent; the measures are new.
- Code: `run11/experiments/common11.py` (add the folder to sys.path). `C.rows()` gives the rows; each row has `clips` (each with `k`, the Hooktheory clip id, and `n_mel`). `C.C5.raw_clips()[k]` is the clip's raw annotation. `C.melody(a)` gives a clip's melody: dict `pitch` (MIDI-like, 12*octave+pc), `dur`, `onset` (beats), `beatpos` (onset mod beats per bar), `bar`, `bpb`, `tonic_pc`; None if fewer than 12 notes. Raw `a["harmony"]` has chords (onset, offset, root_pitch_class, root_position_intervals). `C.baseline(mel)` gives the 13 renaming baselines (ln_notes, density, n_pitch, n_pc, range, int_entropy, n_int, step_share, leap_share, unison_share, pc_entropy, tonic_share, bar_rep). Rows also carry the 17 data-pack measures (mel_ic_mean, mel_h_mean, mel_cheung, ch_ic_mean, ch_h_mean, ch_cheung, mel_rep, ch_rep, and nine rhythm measures).
- **To propose a measure:** write a module `run11/experiments/measures/m_<you>.py` defining `KEYS = [...]` and `clip_measure(mel, a) -> dict` (one value per key per clip; None when undefined). Song value = n_mel-weighted mean over clips. Example: `measures/m00_run10ideas.py` (GAP, S, LOCK). Check it with `python3 checks11.py measures/m_<you>.py <pred> <extra1,extra2>`: songs with a value, clip-1 vs clip-2 stability (different sections of a song, so lower than two transcriptions of one tune; the Hooktheory gate in runs 05-06 was 0.20), overlap with the baselines and pack measures, R² on the 13 baselines, and power by fake outcomes at α .05/6. It takes about 15 s; it reads no outcome.
- **Outcome (held by the chair; never by you):** y_foreign = taken up by another artist (a Discogs-VI version by a performer set sharing no name with the original artist). Model: OLS of z(y) on z(measure) within artist (artist fixed effects) with run 04's controls (chord-count bins, clips per song, ln beats, title flags) and frozen extras; SE about 0.015, so power at α .05/6 is about 0.2 for an effect of −0.03 SD per SD and about 0.7 for −0.05. Second model: conditional logit, sign. Halves: hash of the artist.
- Earlier Hooktheory lessons: chord maps pooled from clips are too small for curvature or the gap (run 03); geometry in a fixed space works better; loop measures mostly count distinct items; the covers study found musicians lean toward "room in the melody" (rests, varied note lengths) and chords that move.
