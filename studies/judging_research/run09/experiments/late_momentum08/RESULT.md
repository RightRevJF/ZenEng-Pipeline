# late_momentum08: result (a lead on seen songs, written after seeing late_window08)

**What was asked.** Is late_window08's lead (+0.051: tunes with more ii-V moves gained more new artists in 2023 to 2024, after ChoCo's charts were collected) only momentum, tunes that were being recorded a lot just before? Rule committed and pushed first (780e2c2): the same model with one more column held, the log of artist sets first dated 2018 to 2022. Predicted +0.03 SD per SD; a lead, not in K.

**What came out** (`result.json`, 8,565 seen songs).
- **ii-V share: +0.044 SD per SD (SE 0.012, p .0002), 95% range +0.021 to +0.067.** Halves +0.040 and +0.044; Poisson +0.072 (p .024).
- Recent momentum itself is strong: +0.37 (SE 0.019). It takes about a seventh of the ii-V estimate (from +0.051 to +0.044), not most of it.
- **Verdict by the rule: lead supported.**

**Readings (deciding nothing).** Within composer +0.049 (SE 0.022, p .023). Outside jazz genres +0.024 (SE 0.017, range −0.010 to +0.058; halves +0.045 and −0.002), unsettled. Without earlier artist sets held +0.079.

**How firm, and what it cannot say.** The lead survives the obvious momentum control, both halves and both models agree, and it holds within composer. It is still on seen songs, written after seeing results, mostly in jazz, in an eighteen-month window that Discogs under-reports. "Recorded by a new artist in 2023 to 2024" is closest to the project's "lasting love" of anything tested so far, but it is a count of recordings, not of listeners. To become a finding it needs songs not yet used or artists after July 2024 (a newer Discogs dump).
