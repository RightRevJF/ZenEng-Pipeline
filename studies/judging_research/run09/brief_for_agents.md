## Rulings in force
- **Power floor (R06-5):** a test counts toward the shared bar (.05 / number of counted tests) only if its power is 0.2 or more, from fake outcomes on the real design before the outcome is joined. Under the floor it is an estimate: passes nothing, kills nothing.
- **Range kill (R07-3):** a counted test is killed only if its prediction lies outside the estimate's 95% range; otherwise a non-pass is "unsettled".
- **Pass:** p < .05/K, predicted sign, same sign in both composer/artist halves (hash of the name), and a second model (Poisson) of the same sign.
- **The maintainer's correction (run 08):** a test written after seeing results is never in the count K; it is reported beside the count as a lead.

## The papers this pass
- `run09/card_1.md` (MATHEMATICS): Chevyrev & Kormilitzin, A Primer on the Signature Method in Machine Learning (arXiv 1603.03788). A path's signature = its iterated integrals; level 2 gives the Lévy area (signed area between the path and its chord, the shoelace formula on the polygon closed by the chord), invariant to translation and time-warping, Chen's identity, shuffle product, uniqueness up to tree-like paths, lead-lag transform (area = half the quadratic variation). The card proposes M1 to M5 (fifths-plane area of chord sets; coupling of the 3rd and 5th DFT phases as a signed area; harmonic arch with time augmentation; tension roughness and arch; quality-leads-root lead-lag) and names the renaming risks.
