# The top note on weak words (749.141): the rule, written before it is run

**Seen before writing:** Review 28's counts in the v43 records, top-note occurrences on weak syllables: song 55, 1 of 3; song 54, 2 of 8; song 58, 4 of 12; song 60, 1 of 4; song 59 (hold-out), 11 of 24. Nothing of the kept songs' figures.

**What is read.** For each kept 4/4 song whose words tie to its notes, the share of its top-note occurrences that fall on a weak syllable (a one-syllable function word or the weak syllable of a longer word; `make_kept_word_weight.tiers`), among occurrences whose syllable the lexicon holds. The same for Luthier's chosen tunes, where a weak syllable is one the lyric does not mark stressed.

**The rule.** If at least two of songs 55, 54, 58 and 60 stand over the kept 90th, it is the engine's, and a change is tried: the planted line is redrawn while any weak syllable in it stands at the peak. If one or none, it is not the engine's as a body and nothing is built; a song over the 90th is flagged in the paper. Song 59 is read and decides nothing.

**What I expect:** kept songs put a fair share of top notes on weak syllables (a top note is often a held or passing note, and a third of all sung syllables are weak); I guess a kept middle near 0.25 and a 90th near 0.6. If so only the hold-out, at 0.46, is high, none of the four is over, and nothing is built.
