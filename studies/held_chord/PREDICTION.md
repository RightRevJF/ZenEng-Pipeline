## A. How kept melodies treat a chord as it holds

**Kept songs** (the kept notes table; every note has its chord). For each note, how long its chord has held: the bars since the chord under the notes last changed (under 1; 1 to 2; 2 to 4; 4 or more). In each band:
- the share of notes on a beat that are tones of the chord (a triad on the chord's root; sevenths not counted);
- the share of notes that repeat the pitch before them.
And for every stretch of four bars or more under one chord: the share of its notes on its commonest pitch.

**The builds** (songs 50, 58, 49 and, beside them, 55: each record's fitted spec, a thousand builds, living builds), read the same way from the notes.

**Rule.** The draw's pull is named wrong under a held chord if:
1. in kept songs the share of on-beat chord tones falls by 0.05 or more from the first band to the last, and
2. in at least two of songs 50, 58 and 49 the builds' share falls by less than half the kept fall.

If kept songs do not fall by 0.05, the pull's steadiness is kept-like and is not named; the stretch figure (the share on a stretch's commonest pitch) is then reported as the measure of the gap and the cause stays with part B.
