# S = 1 + g/pi read on songs — predictions written 19 Nov 2026 before measuring

Jason's master formula: a system's scaling exponent S sits at 1 (criticality, 1/f) plus a coupling g over pi.
Read on a song: S is the DFA exponent of the tune taken as a series, note by note. g = pi (S - 1) is then how far the
song is held off the critical point, and which way (S > 1 smoother, more memory; S < 1 rougher, less).
Three graded sets as before (yardstick G = love + covers); songs with at least 128 notes in one stretch.

 Sp  S of the pitch series (semitones)
 Sr  S of the rhythm series (log time to the next note)
 P1  Sp rises with G                       +     (the old way-2 lead, now on this yardstick)
 P2  nearness of Sp to 1,  -|Sp - 1|       +     (greatness sits at criticality)
 P3  nearness of Sr to 1,  -|Sr - 1|       +
Pass, for each: + in all three sets, p < .05 in at least two, pooled p < .017 (three tests). Controls: year, note count,
source in set 1. Also reported: where the sets' median S sits, i.e. the g of popular song. Nothing goes into the engine.
