# The sound itself — predictions written 19 Nov 2026 before measuring

Store: AcousticBrainz measurements of the recordings in the McGill Billboard sample (848 files; a random draw of
Hot 100 songs, 1958-1991). Smaller than the Spotify table (hundreds of songs, not thousands) but far richer: loudness
and its movement, brightness, roughness, spectral shape, timbre coefficients, beat strength, onset rate, tempo,
chord-change rate, key strength, tuning. Every summary number is used (means and variances; about 150).
Yardstick G = love + covers, each within era, on the songs matched to the Hot 100 with love known.

 A1  a ridge reader over all sound numbers, fitted on one half of the artists and judged on the other
     (both ways, out of fold), against G                                        > 0, p < .05
 A2  AcousticBrainz's own danceability against G (a second measurer of the Spotify finding)   < 0, p < .05
Controls: year, chart peak. Reported besides, as leads only: the single numbers with the same sign in both halves and
the largest pooled reading; love and covers apart. With about 600 songs only readings near 0.1 or more can show.
Nothing goes into the engine on this study.
