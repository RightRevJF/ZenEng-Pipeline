# The sound, second store — predictions written 19 Nov 2026 before the store was read

Second store: Zangerle et al., Hit Song Prediction (Zenodo 3258042): Essentia low-level measurements (the same
extractor as AcousticBrainz, same names) of Million Song Dataset tracks matched to the Hot 100, about 5,800 tracks.
Known weakness, stated first: these are measured from 60-second, 64 kbps, 22 kHz previews, not whole recordings.
That can blur or bend spectral numbers; it can only be judged by whether anything survives.
Set: tracks matched to our Hot 100 table, 2005 or earlier, love known, one per song, every McGill song removed.
G = love + covers, each within era, ranked within this set. Controls: year, chart peak.
