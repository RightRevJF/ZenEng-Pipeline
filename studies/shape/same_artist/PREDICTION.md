# Same artist, great song against other hits — rule written 19 Nov 2026 before measuring

Idea: compare a song only with other songs by the same artist, so fame, voice, era and style cancel.
Songs: sets 1, 2 and 3 (tune measures) and the Hooktheory chord songs of 2012 or earlier (chord measures); artists
with three or more songs. G ranked within each set. Each measure and G are read as differences from the artist's own
mean (artist fixed effects), with year, note count and set held.

21 measures, none new: tune (Sp, Sr, M1 return, M2 varied return, M3 curvature, M4 persistence, M5 room, pace, span,
step size, repeated notes, leaps, long notes, range) and chords (C1-C7). No direction is predicted: every earlier
direction failed, so the test is two-sided.

The lock: artists are split in two by a hash of the artist's name. A measure passes only if it has the same sign in
both artist-halves with p < .05 in each, and pooled p < .0024 (21 tests). Chance should pass about none.
Nothing goes into the engine on this study.
