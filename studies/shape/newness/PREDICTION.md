# Newness and influence — predictions written 19 Nov 2026 before measuring

Idea: greatness as a relation to other songs, not a property of the song alone.
Each tune is the share of its 8-note phrases falling in each of 64 phrase regions (learned without grades).
Three sources, each compared only with itself so transcription habits cancel: Hooktheory, Lakh MIDI, lyrics-melody MIDI.
The pool in each source is every tune with a Hot 100 date (any year, on the yardstick or not), other versions of the
same title left out. Targets: songs on the yardstick (2005 or earlier, love known), with at least 30 pool songs in
each window used.

 N  newness     1 - likeness (cosine) to the mean tune of the ten years before it                    +
 F  ahead       likeness to the mean tune of the ten years after, minus likeness to the ten before  +
Pass, for each: + in all three sources, p < .05 in at least two, pooled p < .025. Controls: year, note count.
Reported besides, no pass rule: T, likeness to its own time (two years either side); G by fifths of N (is there a
best amount of newness); love and covers apart. Nothing goes into the engine on this study.
