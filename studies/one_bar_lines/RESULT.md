- **"No length for the weighty syllables": not named.** The engine gives them more length against the rest than kept lines do (1.5 and 1.7 against a kept middle of 1.0). **What does differ, seen in the tables and not asked by the rule (so a description):**
- **Where the line starts.** A kept line of this size sung in a bar starts after the bar's first beat six times in ten and on it one time in eight. The engine's start on the first beat seven times in ten (song 49) and five in ten (song 54).
- **What it is made of.** Kept one-bar lines are mostly plain eighths with the end held (the commonest: eight eighths; six eighths and a quarter; four eighths and two quarters). Song 49's builds are one figure most of the time, an eighth, three sixteenths, two dotted eighths, a sixteenth and an end (176 and 132 of 633 lines): sixteenths where kept lines have eighths.
- Three of song 49's four one-bar lines and three of song 54's four are lines sung again, so each song's one-bar lines are largely one drawn line, returned.

**What I take from it.** The item supposed the cost of the closer verse was inside the one-bar lines' handling of weight. By this reading it is not. If it is anywhere in these lines it is in where they sit in the bar and in their sixteenths, neither of which the line-by-line fit reads. That would be a new reading with its own rule. It bears only on a form that is off (958 and the kept-shares trial are both off by default since v46), so it changes no song as the engine stands.
