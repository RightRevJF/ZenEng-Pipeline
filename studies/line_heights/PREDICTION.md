# Each line about its own centre, by kept contours: the form and its marks, written before any run (749.201)

26 November 2026. The design is `studies/draw_centre/RESULT.md`; the session's view is the list for v51, addendum 5: part (a) first and alone, the line centres with kept structure and not as noise. Nothing has been run under the form. It is behind a switch that is off (`LUTHIER_LINE_HEIGHTS`).

## What the kept songs gave (read before the form was written; `tables/kept_line_heights.json`)

- 737 kept parts (333 verses, 315 choruses, 55 bridges, 34 pre-choruses), each as the heights of its lines: a line's middle pitch less its part's.
- The spread between lines: verse 0.86, 1.54, 2.38 at the quartiles; chorus 0.74, 1.28, 2.13.
- How one line's height follows the last (the correlation of each line's height with the one before, within a part): the middle kept verse −0.24, the middle chorus −0.34; the 10th to 90th run from about −0.9 to +0.5. With four to eight lines a part, heights drawn with no memory at all would read about −0.2 by arithmetic alone. So kept lines neither glide nor see-saw much: they are close to free of each other, with a part's own shape on top (a verse's highest line late, a chorus's early, a pre-chorus climbing; `KEPT_FOR_THE_DESIGN.md`).

## The form

For each sung part in a build, one kept part of the same kind is drawn (one with the same number of lines where ten or more kept parts have it; otherwise any of the kind, read by place along it). Each fresh line of the part is then drawn about its own centre: the part's centre moved by that line's height in the kept contour, kept two semitones inside the part's floor and top. Nothing else changes: the same register limits, the same weights, the plant (950), fix 964, the gates. A line sung again or copied takes its source's notes as before, and so its height.

This draws whole kept shapes and models nothing. Whatever structure kept line heights have, the form has; it adds no noise of its own making.

## The marks

The power floor (0.2) does not bear here: these are not tests of an effect against chance but readings against kept ranges.

The clean reading (each v50 record's fitted spec, only the form changed, a thousand builds; songs 55, 54, 58, 49, 50, 59):
1. The builds' middle spread between a verse's lines inside the kept middle half (0.86 to 2.38) in every song. (v50: 1.20, 0.94, 0.94, 0.74, 0.70, 1.15.)
2. How one line's height follows the last, the builds' middle, inside the kept 10th to 90th for verses (−0.90 to +0.52) in every song.
3. The middle verse's share of notes on its commonest pitch lower than v50's in at least five songs of six. (The aim for the whole design is the kept middle half, 0.26 to 0.36, and verses over the ceiling at or under 30 in 100 in songs 50, 58, 49. Part (a) alone is not asked to reach it; how far it gets is reported.)

The whole engine, a thousand builds, songs 55, 54, 58, 49, 60, 59, 61, 62 and 50:
4. Living builds no fewer than nine tenths of v50's.
5. The chorus's lift kept: gate 838 passes over no more than 1.15 times the builds it did in v50.
6. The builds' middle words' fit within 0.02 of v50's.
7. Every chosen tune at or over its floor; the peak planted in at least nine tenths of v50's builds.
8. No chosen tune holds a note a beat long or more a semitone from a chord tone (v50: none).
9. The chosen tune's surprise (the record's page-gate reading) at or under the popular-corpus median wherever v50's was.
10. Songs 55, 54, 58: the three-song soundness rule; pools of 10 or more; a random pool pick fails the rule no more often than 6.5 in 100.

All ten on the seen songs, and it goes to the writer to name a sealed song (64 or 65). Any miss and it is not built.
