**Why a note falls under the voice.** I centred the planned band in the voice, and where the band is nearly as wide as the voice (20 semitones in a tenor's 21) that puts its floor on the voice's floor. But a tune dips under its floor: a melisma's second note is the step below, and a line sung again may be set a step under. Left to itself the engine chooses the key after the tune is made, so the dip is always inside the voice. And the planned top is seldom reached (song 66: 67 sung against 68 planned), so the room is at the wrong end.

**Why living builds move.** Not known. They fall in songs 66 and 55 (by 17 and 24 in 100) and rise in song 58 (by 31 in 100). I have not read which of these it is.
