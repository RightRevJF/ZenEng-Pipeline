## Where this leaves the chain, said plainly

Since 749.195 I have proposed six causes for the narrow verse, each from the nearest plausible thing, and tested each by a rule: the low voice, the family's repeated notes, the chord's pull, the tie to the chord, the register's centre, the join. Each was wrong or a part. Three forms were tried and not built. What has been learned is real (the tie is 0.9 where kept is 0.64; the draw is narrow between lines and within them; a line's height does not come from its register; the joins are fine), but guessing the next cause is not working, and I should stop doing it.

**What I propose in its place is a ladder.** Start from the barest draw the engine can make (intervals from the kept table and nothing else) and add its parts one at a time in the order the engine applies them: the register, the degrees, the chord's pull and the clash rule, the weights from the words, the figure memory, the caps on runs, the line-end rules, lines sung again and copied, the gates, the choice. At each rung, read the same five figures against the kept ones (the spread between lines and within them, the share on the commonest pitch, on-beat chord tones, a line's shape from first note to last). The rung where a figure leaves the kept range is where it is lost. No guess is needed, and it will show the whole of 749.200 at once and not a part at a time.

It needs a set of study switches in the draw (each part able to be left out), which change nothing when off, and about an hour of runs. Its rule, written first, would be only this: a rung is named for a figure if that figure is inside the kept middle half before it and outside after it, in four songs of six.
