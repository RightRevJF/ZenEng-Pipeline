# The join between lines: a reading, its rule written first (749.202)

26 November 2026. Seen before writing: `studies/line_heights/RESULT.md`; and, from fix 964's count, that in kept songs intervals of an octave or more are far commoner across lines than inside them. The size of an ordinary kept join, and the engine's, have not been read.

**Kept songs** (`kept_corpus.kept_songs()`; lines as the corpus cuts them), by part: the interval from a line's last note to the next line's first (a join), and the intervals inside lines. For each: the share that repeat the pitch, step (1 or 2), skip (3 or 4), leap (5 or more); and the mean size in semitones. By song: each song's mean join size, at the quartiles.

**The builds** (v50 fitted specs, songs 55, 54, 58, 49, 50, 59; a thousand builds): the same.

**Rule.** The join is named if, in at least four songs of six, the builds' mean join size is under the kept 25th by song, while their mean interval inside lines is at or over the kept 25th by song.

If it is named, the change it points to: a line's first note drawn from the note before it by the kept table of joins, in place of the table for intervals inside a line. Its marks would be written next, before any run.

**What I expect.** Kept joins a good deal wider than kept intervals inside lines (a mean near 3.5 to 4 semitones against 2), and the builds' joins near their inside figure (2 to 2.5). Named. This is the fifth cause I have proposed for narrow verses in three days; the others were each a part. What is different here is that it is not a guess about a weight but a fact about a table (it pools two things the kept songs keep apart), the same kind of thing as 749.192, which held.
