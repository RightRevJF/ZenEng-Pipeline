Seen before writing: a smoke run of 30 builds of song 55 through the first six rungs, to see that the switches work (the barest draw is wider between lines than kept songs, and the chord's pull takes on-beat chord tones from 0.37 to 0.85). ## What it is

Each v50 record's fitted spec, 300 builds a rung, songs 55, 54, 58, 49, 50, 59.

**Up.** The barest draw: a line's notes from the kept interval table (with fix 964) inside the part's floor and top, on the scale, and nothing else; every line fresh; no gates. Then the engine's parts added one at a time, in the order the engine applies them:

register (the pull to the part's centre, 927) → degrees (846) → chord (the pull to chord tones) → clash (808, 833) → moves (the fitted step, leap and repeat weights, 930/936, and 920's lean) → words (847, 880) → memory (923) → runs (926) → end_off → plant (950) → borrow (818: a line sings an earlier line of its part) → again (712, 780: lines sung again and copied by the plan) → lift (839) → gates.

**Down.** The full engine with each part left out alone.

**The figures, each against its kept middle half** (`kept.json`): for verses, the spread between lines (0.86 to 2.38), the spread within lines (1.91 to 2.77), the share of notes on the commonest pitch (0.26 to 0.36), a line's first note less its middle (−1.55 to +0.71), its last note less its middle (−1.88 to +0.21), a line's range (5 to 8), the spread of the lines' first notes (1.61 to 3.70); and for the whole song, on-beat notes that are chord tones (0.53 to 0.73).

The choice is not a rung here (it picks one build); the chosen tunes' figures are read from the v50 records and set beside the last rung.

## The rule

**Up:** a rung is named for a figure if the figure is inside the kept middle half on the rung before and outside on this one, in at least four songs of six.
**Down:** a part is named for a figure if the figure is outside the kept middle half in the full engine and inside with the part left out, in at least four songs of six.
A part is named if either reading names it, and the report says which.

Beside the rule, every rung's step on every figure is reported (the middle of the six songs, and whether it moves toward the kept middle or away), so the size of each part's push shows where nothing is named. The rule can name nothing where the barest draw is already outside, or where one part pushes out and a later one pulls back; the steps are there for that.

The power floor: these are readings against kept ranges and not tests against chance, so there is no power to reckon; with 300 builds a rung the middle of a figure is steady to about its second decimal.

## What I expect

I have guessed six times and been wrong or partly wrong each time, so I write this to be checked and not to be believed. On-beat chord tones: named at "chord" (up), and "again" will raise it further. The share on the commonest pitch: no single rung, a climb over several. The spread between lines: lost at "borrow" and "again", where lines stop being drawn and start being repeated; if that is so, the narrow verse is mostly the plan's repetition and not the draw at all, which would turn the whole chain over once more. A line's shape (starting over its middle): I do not know; perhaps it is there in the barest draw.
