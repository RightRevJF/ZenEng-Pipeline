# One note for half a verse (749.134): the rule, written before it is run

**The worry (Review 26).** A section can sit on one pitch for half its notes without a long run, so the run check passes it. In the v41 records song 58's verses sit on one pitch for 57 notes in 100, song 60's for 46; songs 55, 54, 59: 24, 28, 27.

**Seen before writing:** those five figures, from the review. Nothing of the kept songs' figures.

**What is read.** For each kept 4/4 song, the share of sung notes on the commonest pitch, in its verses pooled and in its choruses pooled (a part with fewer than 20 notes is left out). The same for Luthier's chosen tunes (songs 55, 54, 58, 60; 59 read beside them), as sung, and for the middle of each song's builds.

**The rule.** If, for verses or for choruses, at least two of the four songs' chosen tunes stand over the kept 90th, it is a fault of the engine's and a floor is proposed (to be tried before it is built). If only one does, it is that song's, and is flagged in its record, not gated. If none, the worry is closed.

**What I expect:** the kept 90th for verses near 0.40; song 58 over it, song 60 near it; so one or two songs over, and the rule may land either way.
