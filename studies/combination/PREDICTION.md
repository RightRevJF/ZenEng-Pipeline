# A plain tune in a recording unlike its neighbours? — rules written 19 Nov 2026 before measuring

Songs with both a transcribed tune (the three graded Hot 100 sets) and sound measurements (Spotify's ten readings for nearly all; Essentia's 54 for some).
 plain = the session page reader, frozen (higher = plainer, more like the tunes that are kept)
 far   = the shell: the recording's distance from its era's centre in that era's own metric
Each standardised within its set. Year, note count and chart peak held.
 C1  far alone against the yardstick on these songs: > 0 (it should repeat here).
 C2  plain alone: about zero (it did nothing among hits).
 C3  the combination: the product plain x far, with both held, against the yardstick: > 0, p < .05, pooled over the three sets.
     In words: being far from its era helps a song MORE when the tune is plain.
Reported: the share of great songs (top fifth) in the four corners (plain or not, far or not). If C3 fails, the two are separate facts about different
stages, not a recipe. Nothing goes into the engine on this study.
