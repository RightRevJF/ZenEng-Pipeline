## What is read
The share of a kind's sung notes on its commonest pitch, the kind's sections pooled within a song, a kind of fewer than 20 notes left out (956's own reading, `make_kept_commonest_pitch.share`). Kinds: Verse and Chorus as 956 tells them; **Pre-chorus** (a name with a word starting "pre"); **Bridge** (a name with the word "bridge"). Tags, outros and the rest are not read.

1. **Kept songs** (the 160 kept 4/4 songs): by kind, the number of songs and the 10th, 25th, 50th, 75th and 90th, with the 90th's bootstrap range (songs resampled 2,000 times, seed 0) and the median note count.
2. **The records**: songs 55, 54, 58, 49, 50, 59, 61 (v53 records) and 66 (its record as delivered), by kind.
3. **The builds**: each record's fitted spec, with fixes 966 and 967 on as v53 has them, 300 builds from the lyric's seed, the living builds read as sung: by kind, the median share and the builds in 100 over the kept 90th for that kind. These are builds before the pool's gates, so the verse and chorus rows show what 956 has to pass over.

## The rule
A kind is **named** if, in at least three songs that have the kind, more than 20 living builds in 100 are over the kept 90th for that kind. A kind found in fewer than three of the eight songs (the pre-chorus is in song 66 alone) cannot be named either way and is described only. A kept kind with fewer than 30 songs is read with its bootstrap range beside it and the range's upper end is the line that names.

Naming decides nothing by itself: a named kind goes to the songwriting session as a proposal for a ceiling by kind, which would then need its own trial with marks. Song 59 is read and not fitted to. Song 66 is spent and is read as a seen song.
