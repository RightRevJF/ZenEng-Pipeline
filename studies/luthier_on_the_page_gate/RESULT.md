Reading: all four songs clear a floor set at the kept 10th percentile on plainness as a whole (41st to 78th). One part falls under it: song 55's repeated figures (9th).
All four sit in the lower third of the kept tunes for repeated figures, and all four have a narrower range than three-quarters of them. Song 55 is also near the
floor on surprise (16th). A floor built from this gate would not have changed which of these songs passed; the thing it shows is that Luthier's tunes
re-use their own short figures less than kept tunes do.
