# Instrumental solos and breaks

Report: the Doc "Instrumental solos and breaks for Luthier v8" (https://claude.ai/artifact/15DNkqn3qsUxZM72N5kxxr).

- `solos_billboard.py`: solos and other mid-song instrumental sections in McGill Billboard (via ChoCo): length, place, neighbours, material (shared section letter), lead instrument; per song: solo, any mid-song instrumental, share of the song with a lead voice. Writes `solos_results.json`.
- `solos_pop909.py`: mid-song instrumental sections in POP909's structure labels. Writes `solos_pop909.json`.
