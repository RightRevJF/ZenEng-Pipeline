# The verse lever's range (749.146): the rule, written before it is run

**The worry (Review 29, from song 61).** The verse sung closer acts only on verse lines of 6 to 8 syllables. Song 61's verse lines run 8 to 11, song 59's under 6, so in two of the last three songs the lever does nothing.

**Seen before writing:** the shares the engine already uses, from `studies/metre` (all lines, by tempo band): a line of 6 to 8 syllables takes one bar or less 6, 22, 36 times in 100 (fast, middle, slow); 9 or more, 0, 1, 9. Nothing by single syllable count, and nothing for verses alone.

**What is read** (`study.py`). In the kept 4/4 songs, every verse line: its syllables (notes), and the bars from its first onset to the next line's first onset. The share of lines taking one bar or less, by syllable count 3 to 12, with the count of lines behind each share.

**The rule (the session's).** The lever may take a verse line of n syllables if kept verse lines of n syllables take one bar at least as often as kept 8-syllable verse lines do. The range is whatever that gives. It is then tried as the form was (749.142's checks) before anything is built; this study only says what the range would be.

**What I expect.** The share falls as lines grow. Nine syllables will stand under eight, and ten and eleven well under, so the rule will not widen the range upward; it may confirm 6 to 8 and add 5 and under (which already take one bar by fix 869). If so, song 61's verses are outside what kept songs do in one bar, and its remedy is not this lever.
