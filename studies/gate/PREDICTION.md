# The gate: does plainness separate songs that charted from songs that did not? — rules written 19 Nov 2026 before measuring

Comparing hits with everything else would mostly compare genres, countries and decades. So the contrast is kept inside the artist:
for artists who have both, their songs that reached the Hot 100 against their songs that did not (album tracks, lesser singles). Same voice, same style, same years.
Two sources: Lakh MIDI (whole songs, the voice track guessed) and Hooktheory (sections transcribed by hand). At least 40 notes; artists with at least one of each kind.
The session page reader, fitted on all 23,205 session tunes and frozen, each measure standardised within its source.

 P  the frozen reader is higher for an artist's charted songs than for the same artist's uncharted ones: partial correlation with "charted" (artist and note count
    held) > 0 in both sources, pooled p < .01.
Reported: each part the same way; and the reader without the artist held, for comparison. Caution stated first: which of an artist's songs are in these
collections at all is itself a selection (fans transcribe the songs they know), so "uncharted" here means "uncharted but known enough to be transcribed".
Nothing goes into the engine on this study.
