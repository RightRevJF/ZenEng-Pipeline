Reading: inside one artist's work, the songs that reached the chart are the plainer and more predictable ones: fewer different pitches, a narrower range, smaller steps,
a next note easier to foresee. Small (a twentieth of a rank correlation). With the session tunes (+0.14), the hymn tunes (+0.04 to +0.06) and the null among hits
for covers and love, this is the gate the literature describes: plainness helps a tune get through, and does not rank those that are through.
Caution: the uncharted songs in these collections are the ones fans chose to transcribe.
