# A preordained shape for greatness, measured in a curved space — rules written 19 Nov 2026 before measuring

Jason's proposal: fix in advance the shape the great songs make from their traits, then judge any song by its distance to that shape in a non-Euclidean geometry.
Made honest by a split: the shape is set from one half of the artists and tested on the other half, which the shape never saw.

The space. A recording is a point given by its sound measurements (about 50 Essentia means). The geometry is not flat: distance is measured in the song's own era's
terms. For each song, the hits of its store within three years give a centre and a covariance (shrunk, Ledoit-Wolf); the song's coordinates are its position
relative to that centre in units of that covariance (a Mahalanobis metric that changes from year to year, i.e. a metric that depends on where you stand).
Great = the top fifth of the yardstick G within each store. Ordinary = the rest. Twelve principal directions of the era-relative coordinates are kept (fitted on the defining half).

Four shapes, all fixed from the defining half only:
 S1 the shell      a song's distance from its era's centre (no great songs needed at all)
 S2 the direction  the single direction in which the great lie from the ordinary (Fisher's discriminant)
 S3 the region     two clouds, great and ordinary, each with its own centre and spread; score = how much nearer a song is to the great cloud than to the ordinary one
 S4 the great alone  minus the distance to the great cloud only (the proposal in its plainest form)
Tested on the other half: partial rank correlation with G, year and chart peak held, per store and pooled; and how well each tells the top fifth from the rest (AUC).
 Pass for S3: pooled > +0.10, p < .001, positive in both stores.
 Expected for S4: weak or nothing, because lasting recordings are spread AWAY from the typical in many directions rather than gathered at one point. Stated beforehand.
Benchmarks on the same test half: concentrated spectrum alone; a ridge reader fitted on the defining half.
The same four shapes are then built on Spotify's ten readings for 18,024 hits as a second trial.
Nothing goes into the engine on this study.
