Reading: the great recordings do not gather at a point that other songs could be measured towards. The centre of the great is almost the centre of everybody, and songs
nearer to it are LESS likely to be great. The shape they make is a shell: they lie far from the typical hit of their own years, in that era's own terms, in many
different directions. The shell needs no list of great songs to define it and is the strongest single standard found on the sound so far (+0.144 on an unseen half).
