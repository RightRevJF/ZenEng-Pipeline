# The draw: the two readings

25 November 2026. Rules in `PREDICTION.md` (commit 0094229). Scripts `read_builds.py`, `read_kept.py`; numbers `builds_lyricNN.json`, `kept.json`. Each record's fitted spec, a thousand builds.

## A. The leaps past an octave come from the planted peak (749.182)

| | Kept songs | Song 55 | Song 54 | Song 58 | Song 49 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Builds (songs) holding such a leap, in 100 | 17.5 | 23.6 | 20.0 | 27.4 | 19.6 |
| Such leaps in a thousand notes | 1.5 | 7.7 | 3.0 | 7.2 | 3.2 |
| The far note is the top of the tune, in a chorus | (top: 0.20) | 0.54 | 0.59 | 0.68 | 0.50 |
| In a chorus, of 100 leaps | 44 | 96 | 80 | 94 | 77 |
| **With the plant taken out:** builds holding one, in 100 | | **7.0** | **7.2** | **6.9** | 10.5 |
| leaps in a thousand notes | | 1.2 | 1.3 | 0.8 | 1.6 |
| builds' middle range, with; without | | 17; 13 | 15; 14 | 17; 14 | 15; 14 |

**Both rules hold in three songs of three. The plant (fix 950) is the cause.** Half to two thirds of the leaps have the planted top as their far note, and with the plant out the builds leap *less* than kept songs (7 builds in 100 against 17.5 songs).

Three things I had wrong or had not seen:
- "Twice as often" understated it by notes and overstated it by songs. By builds holding any, the draw is 1.1 to 1.6 times the kept share. By notes it is 2 to 5 times: a build that has such a leap has a dozen of them (kept songs that have one have under three), because the line that carries the peak is copied into every later chorus.
- "Out and back" (749.180) is not quite it. Leaps answered by a leap back within two notes are 17 to 24 in 100, as in kept songs (19). One leap allowed gave nothing back because the leaps come a dozen at a time, in copies.
- The plant cannot simply go: without it the builds' middle range falls to 13 or 14 semitones, which is why it was built.

Kept songs' leaps are another kind of thing: the far note is the song's top one time in five, they fall in verses as often as choruses, and the far note is stressed three times in four and a little longer (a dotted eighth against an eighth).

**The change this points to** is the one written in the prediction: the line that carries the peak is drawn until it reaches the peak and holds no leap past an octave. Its checks are in `PREDICTION_climb.md`, written before it is run.

## B. The weak word held long in a title line is not a fault of the draw (749.183)

| | Kept | Song 55 | Song 54 | Song 58 | Song 49 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Builds (songs) with any such line, in 100 | 18 | 20.6 | 7.4 | 0 | 23.7 |
| Of the cases: a one-syllable function word | 0.94 | 1.00 | 0.49 | | 1.00 |
| the note before the line's widest gap | 0.91 | 0.98 | 0.56 | | 0.98 |
| the line's first note | 0.65 | 0.00 | 0.49 | | 0.48 |
| the longest stressed note within a sixteenth of it | 0.79 | 0.58 | 0.88 | | 0.86 |

By the rule a source is named only if it is at most half as common among the kept cases. None is: the builds' cases are what the kept cases are, a small word before a breath, with a stressed note a sixteenth shorter beside it. **Nothing is named and nothing is tried.**

And the first row corrects what I wrote in v48. **By title line the draw is at the kept rate** (0, 5, 8 and 16 in 100 against 9; by song, 0 to 24 against 18). "A quarter of song 49's builds" was true and misleading: a build has five title lines and a gate takes the build if any one of them fails. The draw has no fault here. The gate was dear because it asked of every build what one kept song in five does not do. Song 55's 16 in 100 is the one figure above the kept rate, and its chosen tune holds none.

So 749.177 closes fully: no gate, and no change to the draw. The session's setting (961) stays for a writer who wants it.

**Against what I expected.** The kind of case: right (function words before a hole). That it marked a fault: wrong. I did not expect the kept cases to look the same.
