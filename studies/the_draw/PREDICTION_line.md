# The peak climbed to, the way back in its own line: checks written before any run (749.182)

25 November 2026. The third form. The writer said to go on with it while Review 34 is being written. Nothing has been run under it.

**Form.** The chorus line that carries the song's peak is drawn 60 times at most. The first draw that reaches the peak with no leap past an octave is kept. If none of the 60 does but one reached the peak, the first such is kept, as fix 950 always did. So a line plants the peak exactly when it would have before, and leaps to it only when it could not climb. The octave gate stays on.

**What I have seen.** Everything: two forms, eight songs, the hold-out among them. No 4/4 song is blind to this question but the two sealed ones (50 and 63), and I leave them sealed: this is a mend to one fix, and a blind song is worth more than that. So this test cannot claim what a hold-out would. What guards it instead is that the marks are the second form's marks, unchanged, with one added that the second form's failure taught.

**The marks.** The whole engine, a thousand builds, in each of songs 55, 54, 58, 49, 60, 59, 61, 62:
1. Living builds above v47's (223, 256, 294, 287, 178, 207, 220, 133).
2. The peak planted in at least 95 builds of every 100 it was in v47 (756, 618, 785, 471, 825, 668, 855, 892); the chosen tune at or over its floor.
3. The builds' middle words' fit within 0.02 of v47's.

On songs 55, 54, 58:
4. The three-song soundness rule; pools of 10 or more; a random pool pick fails the rule no more often than v47's 6.5 in 100.

The clean reading (v47's fitted specs, only the form changed), songs 55, 54, 58, 49, 60, 59:
5. With the octave gate off, the builds holding a leap past an octave at or under the kept share (17.5 in 100).
6. (New.) The peak planted in at least 95 builds of every 100 of v47's on the same spec. This is the form's own doing with the fit held; if it fails here the form does not do what it says.

All six, in every song named, and it is built, on by default. Any miss and it is not built, and I try no fourth form in this window.

**What I expect.** 6 to hold: the line plants on the same condition as before. 2 to hold unless the fits move it; with the plant unchanged, other trials' fits have left its count between 100 and 116 of v47's 100. 1 to hold, by less than the first form (the builds that cannot climb come back with their leaps and meet the gate). 5 is the doubt: about one planted build in six could not climb, which should put builds holding a leap near 12 to 16 in 100, close under the line.
