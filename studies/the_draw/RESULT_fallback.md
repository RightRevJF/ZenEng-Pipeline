## Why the way back did not do its work (749.188)

The clean reading shows it. On v47's fitted specs the peak is planted in 756, 618 and 785 builds of a thousand; under the first form in 630, 514 and 678; under this form in 626, 525 and 684. The way back fired in 2, 12 and 16 builds. I put it at the song's last fresh chorus line, so that every earlier line had its chance to climb. But a line that cannot climb to the peak is nearly always an early one, and by the last line the peak is usually out of reach either way: there was seldom an old-way draw there to fall back on.

The place for the way back is the line itself: draw for the climb, and if none of the 60 climbs but one reached the peak, keep that one. Then a build has its peak exactly when v47's would, and the leap comes back only in the builds that cannot climb, about one in six by the figures above. That is a third form. It was not in the prediction and it is not tried here.
