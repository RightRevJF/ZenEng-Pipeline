It is a straight trade. In about one planted build in six the carrying line can reach the peak only by leaping, and those builds live at a high rate, so they are most of the leaping builds. Refuse them and the peak is planted a sixth less often; allow them and the leaps are back. No rule about which draws to keep can give both, because the draws that would give both are not being made.

So the fault is one step further in than I had it. Fix 950 raises the carrying line's top and leaves its centre where it was. A line centred a sixth or more under its top has to leap to touch it. The mend is to the line's register, not to the choice among its draws: let the carrying line's centre rise toward the peak (as 927 lets a part's centre rise half as far as its top), so that climbing draws are the ordinary ones. That is a different change with its own risks (the chorus's height over the verse, the pitch ceiling of 956, the range floor), and it wants its reading first: where the carrying line's centre sits against its top in kept songs' highest lines.
