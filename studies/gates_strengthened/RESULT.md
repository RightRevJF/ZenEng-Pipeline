What changed in each:
1 page: surprise read from the previous five notes, pitch kept apart from rhythm; about 150 plain measures; ridge and small trees averaged. Rhythm surprise carries nothing.
2 shell: distance to the ten nearest hits of the FIVE YEARS BEFORE, in seven trait families; plus a lean toward the sparse, dark, loose side (that lean borrows from gate 3).
3 sound: concentrated spectrum plus a learned reader of the same kind of sound, each reading ranked within its kind of file. Three near-independent axes:
  concentration (dark, quiet, uncrowded top), harmony that holds still, few events per second. Valence is carried by those, not a fourth axis.
4 devotion: all the sound statistics including how much each changes frame to frame (recordings people return to change little on the surface). Four named axes
  reach the old baseline alone: unhurried, full, dark, steady.
