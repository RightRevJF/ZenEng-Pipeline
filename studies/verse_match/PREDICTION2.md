# The borrows fitted to the aimed song, with the words held: the rule for a second trial

**The first trial (`trial1.json`).** Every song moved nearer its aim, the hold-out too, and its prediction held (song 59's fit chose no borrowing; its gap shrank; half as many builds again passed the gates). It failed one check: song 55's words' fit fell 0.046 (0.03 allowed) when its fit chose to borrow every line. By its rule it was not built.

**The amendment.** The fit may not buy bars with the words. Among the five settings, only those whose trial builds' words' fit is no more than 0.03 under the engine's own setting's (0.8 and 0, on the same trial draws) may be chosen; of those, the one nearest the aim.

**The trial.** New draws again: the fit on one new seed (80 tries a setting, to read the words' fit a little better), the reading on another (150 tries).

**The check is the first trial's, unchanged,** on songs 55, 54 and 58:

1. The summed gap to the aim shrinks in at least two of the three songs and grows by more than 0.05 kept widths in none.
2. The words' fit, whole, falls by no more than 0.03 in any song.
3. At least eight tenths as many builds pass the gates.

**The hold-out's prediction is the same:** song 59's fit chooses less borrowing than 0.8, and its gap shrinks.

Built only if all three hold and the hold-out's prediction holds. This is the second attempt at the same idea; if it fails, the idea rests until there are more practice songs.
