**A clash**, as in the marks: a sung note a beat long or more, off its chord and a semitone from a chord tone.

**Read** (v50 fitted specs, 600 builds, v50 and the pair, the six songs): every clash, by how its line was made:
- **a line borrowed by 818** (kept as a true repeat, so not snapped to the chord);
- **a line sung again or copied by the plan** (a chorus's return, a second verse);
- **a fresh line** (the draw, where the chord tie now stands in for the old pull);
- and apart, whether the note was long **in the draw's own sense** (the last of its line, or a beat or more to the next onset, which is what the clash rule 833 tests) or is long **only as sung** (lengthened afterwards).

**Rule.** A source is **named** if it holds at least half of the rise in clashes (the pair less v50, per 100 notes) in song 55 and in song 49 both. If two sources each hold a third or more in both, both are named.

**What follows.** If the 818 line: the clash rule in `again()` gets a second resort where no chord tone is in reach (move a step away from the semitone), for an 818 line only. If the fresh line: the clash rule in the draw is the place, under the loosened tie. If "long only as sung": the rule tests the wrong length and the mend is to test the sung length.
