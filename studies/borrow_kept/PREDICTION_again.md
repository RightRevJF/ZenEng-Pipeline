# What "sing it again" changes: a reading, its rule written first (749.207, the writer's choice (a))

26 November 2026. Seen before writing: `RESULT.md` here, and the code of `again()` and `join()`. Nothing of this has been counted.

**The question.** When the borrow (818) fires and its line is kept, why is that line so often no longer a repeat of its source?

**What the code can change**, in the order it does so: (1) the count: the new line's syllables differ from the source's notes, so notes are split, dropped or led into (882), and a stressed opening drops a pickup (772); (2) same count, but 882 still drops one note and splits another to sit the words better; (3) a long note that clashes is moved to a chord tone (833); (4) every on-beat note off the new line's chord is moved to a chord tone; (5) a note outside the line's register is moved in; (6) the join moves the first note a step (929).

**Read** (v50 fitted specs, 300 builds, songs 55, 54, 58, 49, 50, 59, verses and choruses together, verses apart): every time 818 fires, whether it fails (drawn fresh), and for each line kept: whether it is a repeat by the measure of `studies/line_repeats`, and which one of these it falls under, taking the first that applies:
- **A. the count differs** (the new line's syllables are not the source's notes);
- **B. the same count, onsets re-fitted** (882 moved notes);
- **C. the same count and onsets, pitches changed**, and then by which step changed the most notes: the chord on the beat, the clash rule, the register, the join;
- **D. unchanged.**

**Rule.** A cause is **named** if it holds at least half of the kept lines that are **not** repeats, over the six songs together, and at least a third in four songs of six. If none does, none is named and the table stands as a description.

**What follows.** If A: the borrow should fire only onto a line of the source's count (or within one), and the kept reading to check first is how far kept repeats differ in count. If the chord step (C): this is 749.198 again (the engine ties every on-beat note to the chord; kept 0.64), and the chord tie comes forward. Marks first either way.

**What I expect.** A named, with 55 to 70 in 100 of the non-repeats; the chord step second, 15 to 25. Lines under D or under A with a count one apart will mostly still be repeats. If the chord step is the larger, I am wrong about what the borrow needs and the chord tie (failed once on other marks, 749.199) is the nearer mend.
