# Pace and the words' fit: result (25 November 2026)

Rule committed first (43c1a84), then the script (before the reading); `result.json`.

**Verdict by the rule: the reading does not hold.** Across 151 kept songs, at the same line-by-line fit, songs whose sections change pace more do not have a lower whole fit: -0.032 (SE 0.050, p .53). With length taken out of salience it is the same (-0.023, p .65).

**What carries the whole fit.** The lines do: whole fit on line fit alone, beta 0.80, R squared 0.64. Pace adds nothing.

**For Window 203's reading.** The whole fit does not mark a change of pace down in kept songs. Song 49 under the trial form runs its verse 1.53 times its chorus, a spread of pace of about 0.21 by this measure, inside the kept songs' range (90th percentile 0.255), where kept songs lose nothing. So the fall of the whole fit under the forms is not the measure counting pace as misfit; it is in how the engine fills a line it gives one bar. A reading for that is proposed as 749.171.

**What it cannot say.** Kept songs are 4/4 chart hits transcribed by ear; the section labels are the corpus's; the pace measure is one of several possible.
