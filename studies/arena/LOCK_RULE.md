# The lock — rule written before the locked half was read (19 Nov 2026)
18 candidates handed back by six researchers; three are the same sound column (tempo locked to one value), so 16 distinct.
Each is run once, unchanged, on the locked artists. Partial Spearman; tunes hold year and note count, per set; sound and hot100 hold year and chart peak
(sound per store). Pooled by Stouffer over the sets or stores where the candidate can be computed (50+ songs).
PASS: the predicted sign in every set or store, and pooled p < .05 / 16 = .0031. `hits_still_to_come` is a fact about the career, flagged by its
own author as a control; it is run and reported but not counted as a finding about music.
