# Does the page reader that works on session tunes pick the chart songs that get covered? — rules written 19 Nov 2026 before measuring

The reader: a ridge over page measures that can be computed the same way on any tune given as notes (range, step size, leaps, stepwise share, repeated notes,
turns, pitch / interval / rhythm variety, long notes, where the top note falls, time on tonic and fifth, ending on the tonic, pentatonic share, thirds,
second half higher than first, compressibility, share of repeated four-note figures, and surprise under a trigram model learned within the same tradition).
The key is estimated the same way everywhere (Krumhansl-Schmuckler). Each measure is standardised within its own collection.
Step 0 (check): fitted on half the 23,205 session tunes it must still read the other half above +0.10; otherwise the portable form is too weak to test.
Then it is fitted on ALL session tunes, frozen, and applied to the 3,124 graded Hot 100 tunes (three transcription sets).
 P1  frozen reader against how much a song is COVERED (cover rank within era), year and note count held: > 0 in all three sets, pooled p < .01.
 P2  against "still loved": expected about zero (love belongs to the record). Reported.
Also reported: the plain parts one by one against covers. Nothing goes into the engine on this study.
