# This Side of the Door (draft 1)

Words and music: Jason Bryant

Verse 1
The TV's been talking since Tuesday
I can see it go blue under the door
Your car hasn't moved since the first frost
And the papers have yellowed out on the porch
I brought you the soup that you like
I've got a key that won't turn anymore
I'm not here to take anything from you
I just want to hear you breathe

Chorus
I've got my ear to the wood
I can hear you in there
Not the words, just the sound of you living
And it's down to one note
Every day that you keep to yourself
It gets harder to hear
Let me in, let me in
I'm on this side of the door

Verse 2
You used to sing the wrong words on purpose
Just to see if I'd laugh
You'd turn the radio up at a red light
And drum on the wheel with both hands
Now you call me at three in the morning
And you don't say a thing
I can hear how slow you are breathing
And I say your name till you hang up

Chorus

Bridge
They tell me you have to want it
That I can't want it for you
Maybe that's so, but I know one thing
Nobody gets well in a room with one chair
I'm not asking you out of there
I'm not asking you for anything more
Than a sound, any sound I can carry home
From this side of the door

Verse 3
The sun's coming up on the street now

I've said every word I could think of

I'll be back when I'm done with work
And I'll knock the way we knocked when we were kids
Three, and then two
