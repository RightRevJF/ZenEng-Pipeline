# This Side of the Door (draft 4)

Words and music: Jason Bryant

Verse 1
The TV's been talking since Tuesday
I can see it go blue under the door
Your car hasn't moved since the first frost
I brought you the soup that you like
I've got a key that won't turn anymore
I didn't bring anyone with me

Chorus
I've got my ear to the wood
I can hear you in there
Not the words, just the sound of you living
And it's down to one note
While the TV is doing the talking
It gets harder to hear
Let me in, let me in
I'm on this side of the door

Verse 2
You sang the wrong words just to make me laugh
And drummed on the wheel at red lights
Now you call me at three in the morning
And you don't say a thing
I can hear how slow you are breathing
And I say your name till you hang up

Bridge
They tell me you have to want it
That I can't want it for you
I've heard it in meetings and waiting rooms
But nobody gets well in a room with one chair
I'm not asking for more
Than a sound I can carry home

Verse 3
My knees have gone stiff on the doormat
And my coffee went cold in the car
I'll leave the soup on the step
And I'll knock the way we knocked when we were kids
Three, and then two

Last Chorus
I've got my ear to the wood
I can hear you in there
Not the words, just the sound of you living
Still one note, but it's there
Every day that you keep to yourself
I'll be here, I'll be here
Three, and then two
On this side of the door
