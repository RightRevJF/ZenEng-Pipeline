# Sadie — lyric draft 1

**Verse 1**
Four seconds left and it's tied at ten,
the bleachers rumble under every boot;
I've chewed my coffee cup down to the rim,
and that's my daughter in the number two.

**Pre-chorus**
I got cut the fall I was seventeen,
never heard my name come off these stands;

**Chorus**
Sadie! Sadie! The home side's on its feet,
two thousand people hollering one name;
I spelled it for a nurse when she was born,
and I never heard it sound this way.

**Verse 2**
She kicked them over the clothesline in the yard,
I held them steady till my hands went raw;
the first day out the boys all laughed so hard,
now the one who laughed the loudest holds the ball.

**Pre-chorus** (as before)

**Chorus** (as before)

**Bridge**
The snap comes back and the band goes quiet,
the holder spins the laces round;
I don't pray much, but my lips are moving,
I'm on my feet and I can't hear a sound.

**Drop** (four bars, the band cut)
It's up.

**Chorus** (as before)

**Last chorus** (the whole crowd)
Sadie! Sadie! The home side's on its feet,
two thousand people hollering one name;
I spelled it for a nurse when she was born,
and it's never gonna sound the same.
