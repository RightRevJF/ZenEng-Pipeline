# Dry Ground (draft 4)
Words and music: Jason Bryant

## Verse 1
Cold little hum of a parked car
Mesh on the glass to hide the day
The quarter pane fought my thumb and gave
And the grey came up and we drifted away

## Pre-Chorus 1
The heater blowing round our feet
The windows running thick with breath
The red brick swimming in the glass
Till the last dry thing went under

## Chorus
Now I'm living on dry ground
Every window painted shut
The sprinkler hangs a rainbow on the lawn
It's never gonna flood again
And I miss the water
Oh I miss the water

## Verse 2
Your back the slow tide of your hips
Pulled me under and up again
Light like honey where your backbone dips
Your skin a garden after rain

## Pre-Chorus 2
You let a little sound get loose
A dove sent out to look for land
She came back with nothing in her mouth
So we went down again

## Chorus

## Bridge
Every day I pass the library
Sitting on its hill like it ran aground
The bird came home with a leaf in her mouth
And Noah stood there missing the rain

## Last Chorus
Now I'm living on dry ground
Every window painted shut
The sprinkler hangs a rainbow on the lawn
I stand out in it till I'm soaked
And I miss the water
Oh I miss the water

## Outro
Some days I park where we used to park
The same cold hum and nobody there
Roll the little window down
Let it in let it in
