# Windows Down — lyric draft 3
Words and music: Jason Bryant

[Verse 1]
The clock still runs four minutes fast,
we always said we'd fix it someday;
I peeled your picture off my locker door
and gave the rest of it away.

[Pre-chorus 1]
Noon, and my shadow's under my boots,
like it doesn't know where to go —

[Chorus]
Windows down till the oil smell's gone,
windows down with the heater on,
Number Four went quiet, but I'm not,
I'm hollering home with all I've got.

[Verse 2]
I shook Mike's hand and held it long,
Sal's still got his hand on the gate;
it gave the same old squeal it always gave,
and I almost asked it to wait.

[Pre-chorus 2]
My thumb left oil on the phone,
you didn't ask me what comes next —

[Chorus]

[Bridge]
I was nineteen when I walked in,
you packed my lunch that first cold day;
they had my hands for twenty-six years,
but you had me the whole damn way.

[Guitar solo]

[Last chorus]
Windows down till the oil smell's gone,
windows down with the heater on,
Number Four went quiet, but I'm not,
I'm hollering home with all I've got.
Windows down till the oil smell's gone,
windows down, but leave a little on;
Number Four can keep the quiet now,
I'm almost home. (held) I'm almost home.
