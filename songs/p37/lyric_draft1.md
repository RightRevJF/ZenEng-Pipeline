# Windows Down — lyric draft 1
Words and music: Jason Bryant

[Verse 1]
Punched my card one final time,
hung my hard hat on the hook;
twenty-six years in a cardboard box,
and I didn't take a second look.

[Pre-chorus 1]
Gravel crunching under my boots,
sun's still high, it's barely three —

[Chorus]
Windows down on the county road,
windows down and the radio low,
the plant is quiet, but I'm not,
I'm singing loud to what we've got.

[Verse 2]
Mike's still sitting in his truck,
Sal won't let go of the gate;
the foreman shook every hand in the lot,
and nobody had much to say.

[Pre-chorus 2]
Called you from the gravel lot,
you said, "Just drive. I'll be here" —

[Chorus]

[Bridge]
I was nineteen when I walked in,
you packed my lunch that first cold day;
I've got two hands, and you've got me,
and none of that got locked away.

[Guitar solo]

[Last chorus]
Windows down on the county road,
windows down and the radio low,
the plant is quiet, but I'm not,
I'm singing loud to what we've got.
Windows down on the county road,
windows down and the radio low,
the time clock's quiet, I'm still on,
I'm almost home. I'm almost home.
