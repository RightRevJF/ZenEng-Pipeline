# I Took Him for the Gardener — draft 2

## Verse 1
It was still dark. I brought the spices;
I knew how to wash the dead.
I came to do the last thing left,
and the stone was moved instead.

## Refrain
I took him for the gardener,
the first one out at dawn;
I said, sir, if you carried him,
just tell me where he's gone.

## Verse 2
I found his linen lying flat,
the cloth that wrapped his head
folded in a place by itself,
as if he'd made the bed.

## Refrain

## (Interlude)

## Verse 3
Two sat in white where he had lain,
one at his head, one at his feet;
they asked me why I cried. I turned,
and someone stood among the trees.

## The Name
He asked me who I came to find;
I said, just give me back my dead.
And then he only said my name.
Mary. And I said, Rabboni.

## Verse 4
God walked the first garden in the evening
and called the man he'd made by name;
I walked this one in the dark, asking where,
and he answered with my name.

## Last refrain
I took him for the gardener,
the first one out at dawn,
the one who walked the first garden,
come back to tend his own.
I took him for the gardener, and he was.
