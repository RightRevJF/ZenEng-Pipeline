He Remembered Today (draft 1)
Words and music: Jason Bryant

Verse 1
He told them of the bend on Route 9
Where the sun hits like a wall
And the light that leaves you blinded
Is still light after all
And the whole church leaned toward him
While his son stood by the door
Reading nothing on the corkboard
Like he'd read it all before

Refrain
He had a light that found the broken
Found the lonely and the strays
And it was always, always light
Only pointed the other way

Verse 2
A boy came by for supper in the spring
Built for every game he played
They went out on the back steps after
And two hours slipped away
His own son stayed at the table
Every night the same
He was never jealous of that boy
Just jealous of the game

Verse 3
He was late to a meeting on a Tuesday
When the boy stood in his door
He said let's pick this up tomorrow
Like there's always one day more
And the boy said sure, no problem
And that night he texted hey
To a son who'd answer in the morning
And the morning was too late

Bridge
At the funeral a phone rang
And he held it out like bread
To a seven-year-old brother
Who just looked at it and said
He doesn't have a phone anymore
And every head was bowed
And the son was in the back pew
And the father saw him now

There's a man who counts the ceiling
Who had watched the father's hand
And he said the door is closing
Not today, but understand

Verse 4
On a Saturday in December
With the cold against the pane
He said what game would you like to see
And he didn't name the game
And the boy could have said nothing
Like he'd learned to say before
But he set his coffee on the counter
And he held open the door

Last refrain
He's going to forget the faces
He's going to lose his way
But the light came round the bend
And he remembered today
