Verse 1
Every Sunday he could hold a room
Like a lantern holds the night
While a boy stood out in the doorway
Just outside of all that light
Some child drew a dove or a dog
On the corkboard by the hall
And no one ever took it down
And no one looked that long at all

Refrain
And the light was always light
It found the lost and found the strays

Bridge

Verse 3
Thirty-eight degrees in December
Two cups cooling side by side
He says what game would you like to see
And he lets the boy decide
And the boy could still say nothing
Like he'd learned to say before
But he sets his coffee on the counter
And he holds open the door

Last refrain
He's going to lose the faces
He's going to lose the way
But the light came round the bend
And he remembered today
