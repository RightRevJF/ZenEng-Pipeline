# He Remembered Today

Words and music: Jason Bryant


## Verse 1
Every Sunday he could hold a room
Every pew would lean in close
And his boy stood back in the doorway
With the words he wanted most
After, his father asked him what he thought
And for once he didn't move on
And the boy said good sermon, Dad
And his father nodded and was gone

## Refrain
He could find the words for every widow
Be the last to leave the grave
And the one who had his name
Got a nod across the nave

## Verse 2
He used to take him to the games
Saturday mornings, just the two
There's a glove still on the closet shelf
That the boy's hand outgrew
Then a text at midnight, only hey
And the boy told himself tomorrow's fine
Now a mother keeps the shoes by the door
Just to trip on them one more time

## Refrain
He could find the words for every widow
Be the last to leave the grave
And the one who had his name
Got a nod across the nave

## Bridge
Now he calls his wife by her mother's name
And his left hand won't keep still
And he asks the doctor, am I still
And the doctor says, for now

## Verse 3
Thirty-eight degrees in December
Two cups cooling side by side
He says what game would you like to see
And he lets the boy decide
And the boy could still say nothing
The way he learned to before
But he sets his coffee on the counter
And he holds open the door

## Last refrain
He's going to lose the words now
One by one they'll slip away
But he found his own boy's name this morning
And he remembered today
