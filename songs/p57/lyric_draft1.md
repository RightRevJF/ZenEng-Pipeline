# The Singer Doesn't Stop (draft 1)
Words and music: Jason Bryant

## Verse 1
My heart keeps a rhythm that wanders but always finds its way home
Every bar of the life You gave me holds the whole of the tune
I've been humming it since morning and I'll hum it through the night
But I know there's a measure coming with a rest that doesn't end

## Chorus
The song can stop
But the singer doesn't stop
You remember every note I ever sang
When the last bar fades to silence
You will sing me again
'Cause the singer doesn't stop

## Verse 2
Lazarus walked out of the dark and back into the same old town
He'd get sick again and die again and they'd lay him in the ground
But You walked out into a morning where the walls can't keep You in
With the nail holes in Your hands so Thomas knew You by Your scars

## Chorus

## Bridge (twice, building)
Not a copy, not a cover
Not a stranger with my face
Every scar and every stumble
Kept and carried into grace
No part of me remembers all of me
But You do, oh You do

## Last Chorus
The song can stop
But the singer doesn't stop
You remember every note I ever sang
When the last bar fades to silence
You will sing me again on a wider stage
'Cause the singer doesn't stop
