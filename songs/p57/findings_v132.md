### 560. 555 was aimed at the wrong place: the joins die at verse 1's fourth line, where the plan sings the third line twice.

**Problem:** v131's 555 said the join deaths came before the second bridge's designed line. That was this session's error: the deaths were never located. Located now, every one is at Verse 1's fourth line. The verse plans ABCCAB and ABCCDE (30% of builds) make the fourth line a return of the third. The third line is drawn fresh with no next line known, so its entry (often a pickup) and its end are drawn without the breath its own return needs; the return then starts too close to it. 555 fits a copy's source only when a designed line follows the copy, so it never touches this.

**Evidence** (main / control):

- Join deaths: v131 113 / 97; v132 142 / 148, all at ('Verse 1', 3).
- 555 fired 501 / 498 times; the bridge's design gate still kills 96 / 98 (v131 84 / 97).
- In a 150-build test, plan ABCC died at the join in 15 of 39 builds; ABCD in none of 111.

**Fix:** When a line's next line returns it (a plan or return of the line just before), draw it so its last onset leaves the breath before its own entry one slot later: last onset ≤ slot bars × bar + its first onset − the breath. Count the lines so drawn.

**Check:** No join deaths at a line that returns the line before it; survivors up by about a seventh.
