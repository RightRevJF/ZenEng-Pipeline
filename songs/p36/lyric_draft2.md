# Dance Anyway (draft 2)

Verse 1
Two a.m., and the dishes are dry,
the microwave's blinking twelve, been blinking since July;
the radio finds an old song I know,
and my socks hit the tile like they've got somewhere to go.

Pre-chorus
Every light in the house is off but one,
and it's blinking like it's counting me in —

Chorus
So I dance anyway,
by the green of a clock stuck on twelve,
one sock sliding on the kitchen tile,
singing all the wrong words, and I mean every one;
yeah, I dance anyway,
like the night's got nowhere else to be,
and the only one watching
is a clock that can't keep time, keeping time with me.

Verse 2
There's a bill on the counter, stamped in red,
a call from this morning still loud in my head;
I know that red, it's the same as July's,
when the lights went out and the clock forgot the time.

Pre-chorus
Every light in the house is off but one,
and it's blinking like it's daring me again —

Chorus

Bridge
Last July I sat here in the dark,
waiting for the lights to tell me I could start;
I could hear the neighbors' radio through the wall,
and I didn't move at all.

Last chorus
So I dance anyway,
by the green of a clock stuck on twelve,
with the bill turned face-down on the counter,
singing all the wrong words, and I mean every one;
yeah, I dance anyway,
like the night's got nowhere else to be;
it can stay twelve o'clock forever —
I'm still here, so I dance anyway.
