# Dance Anyway (draft 1)

Verse 1
Two a.m., and the dishes are dry,
the fridge hums low with its door half wide,
the radio finds an old song I know,
and my socks hit the tile like they've got somewhere to go.

Pre-chorus
Nobody's up to see it,
nobody's keeping score —

Chorus
So I dance anyway,
spin by the sink in the kitchen light,
hip against the counter,
singing all the words I never get right;
yeah, I dance anyway,
slide past the stove in the fridge's glow,
the whole house sleeping
and the whole wide world doesn't have to know.

Verse 2
There's a bill on the counter, stamped in red,
a call from this morning still loud in my head;
nothing got fixed and nothing got paid,
but the bass line's walking and my feet won't stay.

Pre-chorus
Nobody's up to see it,
nobody's keeping score —

Chorus

Bridge
They say wait till the trouble's gone,
till the money's in and the lights stay on;
I have waited before, and it wears you thin,
so I turn it up and I let it in.

Last chorus
So I dance anyway,
spin by the sink in the kitchen light,
hip against the counter,
singing all the words I never get right;
yeah, I dance anyway,
slide past the stove in the fridge's glow,
the bill's still on the counter,
and I'm still here, so I dance anyway.
