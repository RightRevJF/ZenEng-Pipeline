## New findings

### 749.76 887 gives the rest its kept length by cutting the line's last note
**Problem.** 887 draws the last note's length from the kept shares, but only among lengths that leave a quarter's rest before the next line. In a two-bar slot the long kept lengths rarely fit, so they drop out and the short ones take their share. The rest reached the kept median by taking time from the held word.
**Evidence.** Line-final notes (lines that end without a melisma, the last syllable's own length, as the kept table counts them). Stressed, an eighth or less: kept 0.22; songs 54, 55, 58: 0.29, 0.54, 0.44. Stressed, a half or more: kept 0.31; 0.29, 0.03, 0.11. Weak syllable, an eighth or less: kept 0.40; song 55 0.60, song 58 0.85. Function word, a half or more: kept 0.22; 0.08, 0.00, 0.00.
**Fix.** Draw the last note and the rest after it together, from the kept pairs for the syllable's weight. Where the slot cannot hold both, give the line its second bar (869), or let the next line enter later, before shortening the note.
**Check.** By weight, line-final lengths within ten points of the kept shares at both ends (an eighth or less, a half or more), with the rest still inside the kept quartiles.

### 749.77 891 is not enough in song 54
**Problem.** The charge for a stressed syllable left a sixteenth (4) does not bring song 54's lines sung again down to its other lines.
**Evidence.** `review9_stressed16.py`: song 54, lines sung again 14 of 131 stressed syllables a sixteenth long, its other lines 5 of 82 (v22: 19 of 134). Songs 55 (12 of 79 against 11 of 73) and 58 (6 of 72 against 9 of 111) pass.
**Fix.** Raise `short_stress` until song 54 passes, or forbid the split that leaves a stressed syllable a sixteenth when another split of the same count exists.
**Check.** In all three 4/4 songs, stressed sixteenths in lines sung again at or under the same song's other lines.

### 749.78 871 is a weight, not a rule
**Problem.** 871 and 890 add or take 1.5 from a line's score, so a line can still end with the weak syllable held longer than the stressed one. 887 caps the weak last note but does nothing for a stressed syllable left a sixteenth before it.
**Evidence.** Song 55 under v24: "summers" at a line's end, stressed syllable 3 twelfths, weak 6 (under v22, 0 of 6 such endings).
**Fix.** In 887's pass, where a line ends on a word's weak syllables, give the stressed syllable at least a quarter (or the weak no more than the stressed), as a rule.
**Check.** No line-final word holds its weak syllable longer than its stressed one, in any song.

### 749.79 892 leaves a third of song 54's sung notes with no guitar; the old check still reads guitars against a piano rule
**Problem.** With the seconds either side of the sung note struck out of the guitar's register, some chords have no three-note voicing left, and the fallback (under the tune) has no room either, so the guitar plays nothing there. Separately, "sung notes with accompaniment above them" is still printed against 749.21's 2 in 100, which was measured on piano Lieder.
**Evidence.** Song 54: the guitar sounds under 330 of 503 sung notes (0.656); the 173 without it sit on C4, B3 and D4 (49, 49, 41 of them), none in a strum's own gap. Accompaniment above a sung note: 0.441, 0.973, 0.917 in songs 54, 55, 58.
**Fix.** When no voicing passes, take the chord with the clashing tone left out (two notes), or the same voicing an octave down, rather than nothing. Print the above-the-tune share for piano only, or against a kept guitar figure if one is measured.
**Check.** The guitar under at least nine sung notes in ten in every 4/4 song with a guitar lead; the check reads only parts it was measured for.

### 749.80 The voice now breathes after nearly every line
**Problem.** With 887 every line ends in a rest of about two beats, so the voice stops more often than kept singers do; kept songs run some lines into the next.
**Evidence.** `studies/breath/silences.py`. Silences of a beat or more per 100 beats of the sung span: songs 54, 55, 58 12.3, 14.0, 13.0 (v22 12.1, 13.3, 12.7); CoCoPops Billboard, 205 songs, quartiles 7.0, 9.1, 11.1. Median silence now 2.5, 2.0, 2.0 beats (v22 1.0 in all three); CoCoPops 2.0. By onsets, song 58 takes 7.6 real breaths a minute (human median 7).
**Fix.** Let a line run into the next at the kept rate: measure the kept share of line joins with less than a beat of silence (`kept_corpus.kept_songs` splits lines at a half note) and draw it in 887's pass.
**Check.** Silences of a beat or more per 100 beats inside the kept quartiles; median silence inside the kept quartiles (1.5 to 3.0 beats).
