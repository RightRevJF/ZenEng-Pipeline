# Same Time Tomorrow (draft 1)
Words and music: Jason Bryant

## Verse 1
The boy who changes my sheets don't waste a single move
He tucks the corners tight like he's got nothing left to prove
There's a pear tree past the window standing bare against the gray
I started on one story and he didn't walk away
He came back the next morning, came in way too soon
Pulled the chair up and said go on, my shift ain't till noon

## Chorus
Same time tomorrow, he says it at the door
Same time tomorrow, I say it back once more
It's a little thing you promise when there's nothing left to say
But he keeps it and I keep it one more day

## Verse 2
I had five hundred seats of oak beneath a roof of old gray stone
And twelve folks every Sunday, each one sitting on their own
I climbed up to that pulpit sure the crowd was on its way
And the crowd never came but the twelve came anyway
There's a woman tending bar who slips her own cash in the till
Twelve years she's never said a word and I don't guess she will

## Chorus 2

## Bridge
Every August at the beach there's eight gray wooden stairs
And at the top the ocean's all at once already there
It doesn't know my name and it don't care what I bring
The summer that we lost my sister it didn't change a thing
It took me in exactly like it took me every year
And I believe what's next is that, except it knows I'm here

## Verse 3
Friday he'll come early and he'll find the bed made up
Nobody in it needing nothing, no water in the cup
He'll pull the chair back to the wall and look out at the tree
And say something to the window that ain't for you or me

## Last Chorus
Same time tomorrow, I told him at the door
Same time tomorrow, I'll just get there before
And when he climbs up to the top some morning far away
I'll be there like the water and I'll keep it anyway
