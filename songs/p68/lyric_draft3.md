# Same Time Tomorrow (draft 3)
Words and music: Jason Bryant

## Verse 1
The boy who comes to change the sheets
does it like he learned it young
every corner folded under
nothing wasted, nothing wrong
I told him one old story
just to keep him in the room
and he came back the next morning
hours early, hours too soon

## Chorus
Same time tomorrow
it's the smallest thing we swear
not carved above a doorway
not lifted up in prayer
but it's held up more of heaven
than my steeple ever bore
same time tomorrow
and his hand upon the door

## Verse 2
Seven years I climbed that pulpit
sure the crowd was on its way
five hundred seats of oak below me
and twelve people, and they stayed
each one in the pew they'd chosen
like a claim on quiet ground
I gave them precious little reason
and they kept on coming round

## Chorus 2

## Bridge
Every August there's a walkway
eight gray steps above the sand
and at the top the whole sea opens
wider than a man can stand
The summer that we lost my sister
it took me in no less
than every year before it
It has never known my name
and I believe the next one does

## Verse 3
The boy asked was I frightened
I said not of where I'm bound
a little of the getting there
the long way going down
Friday he'll come early
to a bed made up and still
he'll set the chair back by the wall
and tell the window what he will

## Last Chorus
Same time tomorrow
I told him at the door
for once I'll be the early one
I'll get there just before
and when he climbs up to the top
some morning years from now
I'll be waiting like the water
and I'll know his name somehow
same time tomorrow
