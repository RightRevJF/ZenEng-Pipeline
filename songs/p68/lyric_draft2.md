# Same Time Tomorrow (draft 2)
Words and music: Jason Bryant

## Verse 1
They built the church for hundreds
and it holds a dozen now
each in the pew they've worn to fit them
none of them could tell you how
or why they keep on coming
to a sermon dull as rain
the preacher waits on thousands
and the dozen come again

## Verse 2
There's a man who drives his neighbor
to the clinic every week
sits an hour with a magazine
he doesn't try to read
he's never once been thanked for it
he'd flinch if he were thanked
he says I'll see you Thursday
and he's there, and that is that

## Chorus
Same time tomorrow
it's the smallest thing we swear
not carved above a doorway
not lifted up in prayer
but it's held up more of heaven
than the steeple ever bore
same time tomorrow
and a hand upon the door

## Verse 3
In a room as clean as nothing
where the sheets are changed at nine
a boy who's paid to change them
keeps on staying past his time
an old man tells him stories
like he's paying down a debt
and when the boy gets up to go he says it
and he hasn't missed one yet

## Chorus 2

## Bridge
We spend our lives afraid we'll miss it
the sky that splits in two
the morning that the crowd comes
and the thunder rolling through
but it comes in on a Tuesday
in somebody's muddy boots
with a sack of something from the store
and nothing much to prove

## Verse 4
Friday the bed is empty
and the blanket's folded square
the boy comes in at nine like always
and he puts away the chair
he says something to the window
that nobody gets to hear
and the dozen will be sitting in their pews
come Sunday, right on time, right here

## Last Chorus
Same time tomorrow
and one tomorrow comes
with no morning in the window
and no Sunday and no sun
and maybe someone's waiting
like the ocean past the stair
that never needed us at all
and is glad that we are there
same time tomorrow
