# Swallowed Up (draft 1)

Verse 1
The straps creak out their slack into the clay,
the tent is drumming with November rain;
they let him down hand over hand, by inches,
my father, in the suit he wore to pray.
I hold his Bible, margins rubbed to cloth,
the verses he had underlined in lead;
he taught me every word I have to say,
and now I say them, standing at his head.

Chorus
Swallowed up!
The mouth of earth is open wide,
the grave that never says "Enough"
has swallowed him up.

Verse 2
I stoop and fill my fist with the wet ground,
it's cold as iron, heavy as a debt;
"Earth to earth," I tell him, "ashes, dust,"
and throw it, and the lid gives back the sound:
a knock on the door at three in the morning,
the sound since Abel's blood cried from the field;
my mother turns her face into my coat,
the backhoe idles, waiting by the hedge.

Chorus

Verse 3
I've said these words above you twenty years;
you've never answered, never once said no.
You took the old, the stillborn and the brave,
and opened wide again before the snow.
You've had them all since Eden's gate was barred,
you're full of kings and beggars, row on row,
but what you've got down there is not your own,
and you're the one who's going to let him go.

Chorus

Solo

Slowdown (half speed)
O death, where is your sting?
O grave, where is your victory?
You'll split like a seed in the ground
and give him up at dawn.

Riff

Last chorus
Swallowed up!
Death is swallowed up in victory;
the mouth that never said "Enough"
is swallowed up!
