## Findings against v64

1. **The songbook split (the writer's item, already on the song 21 fix list).** Done by hand here: a musicians' songbook and this report. The consistency check now tests the songbook for engine terms (ZenEng, module, certificate, survived, composite, floor, version numbers).
2. **A true duet has no harmony rule.** v58–v59 give a second singer a row and an ensemble kind ("in parts"), but nothing says how a harmony line is derived from the melody: which interval, whether it must be a chord tone, voice crossing, the final-chorus allowance under the lead's. The run set his part a third below on chord tones, else a sixth. 11.2/27 could state that rule and check it.
3. **The joins across two singers.** The run's join pass treated both singers as one line, so a pickup from one couldn't overlap the other's last note. That killed 677 candidates, the largest cause. In a duet a hand-off may overlap. Joins should be checked per singer.
4. **Calibration across three keys.** Her verses (E minor), his (F minor) and the chorus (C major) are read against one tonic (C). The calibration could read each section in its own key, as the suite fields already carry it (v59).
5. **27.21 in an additive metre.** The build's fills are written in beats ("2-beat, 1-beat, 0.5-beat"), but a 7/8 bar's beats are uneven (2+2+3). The run used eighths (4, 2, 1). 27.21 should define the beat in additive metres.
6. **27.21's floor can't see a static line.** The first lines passed every row (chord tones, three or more distinct pitches, the peak's place, rising density) while oscillating around one note. A motion row (each phrase spanning at least a fifth, or a ceiling on repeated-note share) would catch it.
7. **Negation focus.** In "and I am not afraid," the lexicon stresses only "-fraid," so the walk couldn't hold it on the downbeat. Sentence negation ("I am NOT afraid") carries focus. v58's stress rules could add it, printed when it fires.
