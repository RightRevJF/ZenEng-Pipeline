## What the song is about

Two worlds touch along a line that won't close. She is on one side. Her world is a grey building with one warm room at the bottom of the stairs, a crack in the floor, an instrument worn smooth where her hands rest, and his lit window seen through her glass. He is on the other side, at his window every morning: the coffee right, the light right, and the sky wrong by a degree, where one blue stops and another starts.

The crack in her floor and the seam in his sky are the same wound. They can both see it. Neither can cross it.

They never sing a verse together. They meet only in the chorus, at the seam, his voice under hers. After the instrumental, where his world comes apart, the last chorus turns: "the seam won't close, and neither will we." Then he is alone. He stops measuring, leaves his notebook on the sill, and says the last thing: fear requires surprise, and he is not afraid.

**Playing it:**

- **The keys are part of the story.** Her E minor and his F minor sit a half step apart, like his two blues "misaligned by a degree." The C major chorus is the one place both keys can reach.
- **Play the verses small and the choruses wide.**
- **Let the instrumental be the city coming apart:** it starts thin and ends with everything.

## The song, section by section

Each lyric line takes two bars; the chords of both bars are shown beside it.

### Intro · bars 1–8 · E minor

*Em C · Am Em · Em C · Am C.* The synth line alone over the pad for four bars, then half-time drums enter. The line states the chorus's opening shape: a lift of a third into a held note, a drop, a step up.

### Verse 1 · bars 9–16 · E minor · **her**

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| Em · C | six inches of crack in a grey that never breaks |
| Am · Em | one warm room at the bottom of the stairs |
| Em · C | my hands on the worn place in the wood |
| Am · C | your window, lit, beyond my glass |

- **Melody:** low and close, between A3 and D4, almost spoken. "Stairs" and "wood" hold on C4.
- **Delivery:** intimate, unhurried. She is describing a room she knows by heart.
- **Band:** half-time (kick on 1, snare on 3, quiet eighth hats), clean guitar arpeggios, Rhodes, the pad, the bass on 1 and 3. The C chord in bar 16 is the hinge into his key.

### Verse 2 · bars 17–24 · F minor · **him**

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| Fm · D♭ | the coffee's right, the light is right |
| B♭m · Fm | the sky is wrong by a degree |
| Fm · D♭ | one blue stops where another starts |
| B♭m · C | I write it down and nobody looks up |

- **Melody:** A♭3–D♭4, plain and observational. "Degree" holds on A♭3.
- **Delivery:** matter-of-fact, a careful man noting something precisely.
- **Band:** the same groove, a half step higher. The bass adds the fifth on beat 4, and a muted guitar plays on the quarters in place of the arpeggios. The C in bar 24 is the dominant that opens the chorus.

### Chorus 1 · bars 25–32 · C major · **both**

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| C · G | a seam across the sky |
| Am · F | two blues, and neither one is wrong |
| C · G | a line we both can see and neither cross |
| F · C | the seam won't close, the seam won't heal |

- **Melody (her):** "a" is a pickup, and "SEAM" lands on G4 on the downbeat of bar 25 and holds. That's the top of the chorus, the fifth of C. "Sky" holds on E4 for most of a bar.
- **Harmony (him):** a third below her throughout, a sixth where the third isn't in the chord. On "SEAM" he sings E4 under her G4.
- **Band:** full time (kick on 1, the "and" of 2 and 3; snare on 2 and 4; ride), overdriven guitar in eighths, crash on bar 25.

### Interlude · bars 33–36 · C major

*F C · Am Am.* The synth line over Rhodes and pad; the drums drop out. The Am leads back into her E minor.

### Verse 3 · bars 37–44 · E minor · **her**

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| Em · C | I made a door; it only opens one way |
| Am · Em | the light in your window never goes out |
| Em · C | there's a note on the keys: trust the third layer |
| Am · C | every day I stay, the edges thin |

- **Melody:** a little higher than verse 1, up to E4. "Thin" holds on C4.
- **Delivery:** the line about the note on the keys is quoted: she is reading it.

### Verse 4 · bars 45–52 · F minor · **him**

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| Fm · D♭ | I kept the record against the day |
| B♭m · Fm | the seam has moved a hand's width east |
| Fm · D♭ | this morning I put the pencil down |
| B♭m · C | the smell of bread came through the open door |

- **Melody:** F3–D♭4. "Down" holds on C4.
- **Delivery:** this is where he changes. The first two lines are still measuring; "I put the pencil down" lets go; the bread is ordinary life coming in. Soften from the third line on.

### Chorus 2 · bars 53–60 · C major · **both**

As chorus 1. The last C (bar 60) is the dominant of the instrumental's F minor.

### Instrumental · bars 61–108 · 7/8 · F minor · **synth lead**

*Fm · D♭ · B♭m · C, one chord a bar, twelve times.* The bars are 7/8, felt 2+2+3, with the quarter note at the same speed as the song.

**The synth line** is written out as a cue on the lead sheet. It takes the chorus's opening shape and builds in three stages:

- **Bars 61–76:** long notes, the shape stated broadly.
- **Bars 77–92:** quarters, the shape moved up a step.
- **Bars 93–108:** running eighths, climbing to C6 in the last bar.

**The band builds under it:**

- bars 61–72: synth, pad and Rhodes only;
- from bar 73: the bass (2+2+3: two quarters, then three eighths);
- from bar 85: drums (kick on the first and third groups, snare on the second group and the middle of the 3-group);
- from bar 97: everything, the ride and the overdriven guitar;
- a tom fill in bar 108.

The intensity should rise all the way.

### Chorus 3 · bars 109–116 · C major · **both**

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| C · G | a seam across the sky |
| Am · F | two blues, and neither one is wrong |
| C · G | a line we both can see and neither cross |
| C · F | the seam won't close, and neither will we |

- **The turn:** the last line is new. It climbs through "neither" to "will" on G4. Then "WE" lands on C5 (her) over A4 (him) on the downbeat of bar 116, over F, and holds. These are both singers' highest notes in the song.
- **Band:** everything, with a crash and china on bar 116. The F chord turns straight into his F minor.

### Ending · bars 117–122 · F minor → F major · **him alone**

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| Fm · D♭ | I leave the notebook on the sill |
| B♭m · C | fear requires surprise |
| D♭ · F | and I am not afraid |

- **Melody:** low (F3–C4), each line ending on a long note: "sill" on F3, "surprise" on C4, "afraid" on A3.
- **Delivery:** "and I am not afraid" is spaced out, word by word, toward the held "-fraid" on the downbeat of bar 122. The A3 is the major third of the final F chord: the minor key turns major on his last word.
- **Band:** drums out; pad and Rhodes; one bass note a bar.

### Outro · bars 123–130 · F minor → F major

*Fm D♭ · B♭m C · Fm D♭ · C F.* The synth line over pad and Rhodes, ending on A (the third of F). The last bar is an F major chord, left to ring.

## For each player

**Drums**

- **Verses:** half-time (kick on 1, snare on 3), quiet eighth hats.
- **Choruses:** full time (kick on 1, the "and" of 2 and 3; snare on 2 and 4), on the ride; crash at the top of each chorus.
- **Instrumental (7/8, 2+2+3):** out until bar 85. Then kick on the first and third groups, snare on the second group and the middle of the 3-group, with eighth hats changing to the ride at bar 97. A tom fill in bar 108.
- **Ending:** out. **Outro:** one crash on the final F.

**Bass**

- **Her verses:** roots on 1 and 3.
- **His verses:** roots on 1 and 3, plus the fifth on 4.
- **Choruses:** quarter notes on the root.
- **Instrumental:** enters at bar 73 with a 2+2+3 figure (root, root, then three eighths with the fifth in the middle).
- **Ending:** one note a bar.

**Guitars**

- **Her verses:** clean, ringing eighth-note arpeggios.
- **His verses:** a muted note on each quarter, high.
- **Choruses and the last quarter of the instrumental:** overdriven root-fifth-octave chords in eighths.

**Keys**

- **Rhodes:** chords on 1 and the "and" of 2 throughout (on 1 and the 3-group in 7/8).
- **The synth pad:** under the whole song, louder in the choruses and the instrumental. It's the sound of the world she built, so keep it warm and wide.

**Synth lead**

The intro, interlude, instrumental and outro lines are written out as cues on the lead sheet:

- **Patch:** a warm saw lead.
- **The motif:** the chorus's opening shape ("a SEAM a-cross": up a third into a held note, a drop, a step up).
- **The instrumental:** goes from long notes to quarters to eighths, climbing to C6.
- **The outro:** ends on A.

## For the singers

**Her: A3–B4, C5 once.**

- **The verses:** low and conversational, A3–E4.
- **The chorus melody is hers.** "SEAM" on G4 is the held top; "sky" on E4 holds nearly a bar.
- **The last "WE" on C5 is a close vowel ("ee") at the top of the range.** Open it slightly toward "ih" and support it. It is the song's peak.
- **Breathe** at each line end. Every line has at least a beat of air after it.

**Him: E3–G4, A4 once.**

- **His verses:** A♭3–D♭4, plain and exact.
- **In the choruses he sings under her,** a third below (a sixth here and there). His part is written on the lead sheet with the words.
- **His A4 comes once,** under her C5 on the last "we."
- **The ending is his alone and should be the quietest singing in the song.** "And I am not afraid" goes word by word to the held A3.

**Both:**

- **The two keys change under you.** Her verse ends on C (bar 16), and his starts in F minor in bar 17. His verse ends on C (bar 24), and the chorus opens in C. Listen for the C chord as the hinge each time.
- **The quarter note never changes,** including through the 7/8.

## Arrangement and production notes

- **The sound world:** warm instruments (pad, Rhodes, clean guitar) inside a grey, steady pulse. The overdrive is saved for the choruses and the end of the instrumental.
- **Her side and his side** should sound slightly different. Arpeggios and a still bass under her; a muted guitar and a moving bass under him.
- **The instrumental is the drama.** Start it almost empty, so the build has somewhere to go.
- **After the instrumental, the last chorus is the biggest moment.** The ending drops to almost nothing straight after it.
- **The final F major chord should ring out:** his F minor, turned major at last.
