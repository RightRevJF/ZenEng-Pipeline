## The fixes

### 298. On the default path the chorus can sit under its verse, and since 296 nothing in the pick prefers otherwise.

**Problem:** the writer asked for a world-class hook and took the default lift (Q17 A). 288 draws the chorus's median, top and lowest from the kept distribution, where the chorus top is at or under the verse's in half of kept songs, and 296 took the lift mark out of the composite's fit term, the only term that preferred a lift. 

**Evidence:** the pick's registers were drawn at the kept 28th percentile (chorus median +0.12, top -0.88) and read -1 and -2: the chorus tops at G4 under the verse's A4, and the hook runs C♯4–G4 and lands on D4. Under the old fit term the pick is 353742341ffc (lift +4.5 median, +2 top). Only 41% of main's builds have the chorus median inside the kept middle half (288's check asks half). 

**Fix:** the intake's lift question prints what the default draws (a chorus top at or under the verse's in about half the builds) before the writer answers; the paper reads, on kept songs, where the title line sits against the verse, and if it sits above in most, the title line's register becomes an aim. 

**Check:** the next default-path intake shows the figure; the report prints the title line's median against the verse's beside the kept figure.

### 299. The judge's floor is the search's largest killer; the builds sit far under the kept held-out percentiles, and the chorus's design is what it reads.

**Problem:** the refitted judges (291) put a floor at the kept 5th percentile, and a quarter of this song's builds fall under it. 

**Evidence:** deaths at the floor: main 164, control 141; under the floor 26.5% (main) and 21.3% (control) of complete builds, against 5% of kept windows by its making; combined median -0.13 against the kept 2.95; 0.1% of builds reach the kept median. The heaviest give-away is the spread of notes per bar (-1.255): the chorus window reads 3.53 at the median against kept windows' 1.60 (95th 2.91). The chorus's two-bar lines put nine to eleven syllables in one bar and the held title in the next, the writer's design (Q21) and 232's hold. 

**Fix:** where 230's two-bar lines are a named design, the floor reads the window outside the designed section, or the design is printed with the judge's reading of it and exempt from the floor. 

**Check:** deaths at the floor under one build in ten when a section is a 230 design.

### 302. Endings: the move 295 meant to retire still fires in one build in five, and the last chorus misses the tonic in one in ten.
