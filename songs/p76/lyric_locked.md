# They Did the Right Thing (locked)

Words and music: Jason Bryant

## Verse 1
Third level of the parking garage
Her jacket pressed against the passenger glass
Two badges, two different floors upstairs
And something neither one of them could ask

They didn't name it, just showed up again
Drove the same deck every other week
Found what was there inside the borrowed dark
And left their phones face-down across the seat

## Pre-chorus 1
Sunday mornings, back in separate pews
The same prayers, the same handshake, same amen
But every phone was charged and on the table
And they both knew where they'd been

## Chorus 1
They did the right thing
Like decent people should
Walked away from what was living
Went home and stayed for good
Some things done in daylight
Still bleed when you move on
You can do right by your people
And still be gone

## Verse 2
Came in on a Tuesday, no appointment
Just stood inside the doorway, wouldn't sit
Said he'd read her phone, read every message
Said end it now, or I'm the one who quits

He wasn't loud, the quiet was the harder part
Just set his hands out flat and held the frame
Said he'd forgive her if they called it over
Then turned and walked back out the way he came

## Pre-chorus 2
Sunday mornings, back in separate pews
The same prayers, the same handshake, same amen
And every phone lay silent on the table
And neither looked again

## Chorus 2
They did the right thing
Like decent people should
Walked away from what was living
Went home and stayed for good
Some things done in daylight
Still bleed when you move on
You can do right by your people
And still be gone

## Bridge
Her husband held her like he meant it
His wife asked nothing, held him all the same
The good ones loved the broken people back
And that's the part that doesn't have a name

## Final chorus
They did the right thing
The way the good ones knew they would
Walked away from what was living
Went home and stayed for good
Some things done in daylight
Still bleed when you move on
You can do right by your people
And still be gone
