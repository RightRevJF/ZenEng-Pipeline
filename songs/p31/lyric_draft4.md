# Nothing On — draft 4

## Verse 1
ten-fifteen and the whole street blinks
the fridge goes quiet and the fan spins down
my phone's on nine and I'm letting it die
the only thing glowing is you

## Pre-chorus
the air went off and the room got warm
so quiet I can hear you breathe

## Chorus
nothing on, nothing on
everybody's waiting on the lights
I hope they never come back on
nothing on, nothing on

## Post-chorus
nothing on but the moon
(nothing on) nothing on but you

## Verse 2
found a candle from your birthday in the kitchen drawer
you struck a match and pulled me to the floor
you told me things you'd never say with the lights on
funny what you see when there's nothing to see

## Pre-chorus 2
the candle gave out and I heard you smile
say my name till I find where you are

## Chorus 2
nothing on, nothing on
can't see a thing, so we'll go by feel
and if they come back, I'll turn them off
nothing on, nothing on

## Post-chorus
nothing on but the moon
(nothing on) nothing on but you

## Bridge
two a.m. and the fridge clears its throat
every window on the street opens one eye
the microwave's blinking like it caught us
my hand finds the switch and I hold my breath

## Final chorus
nothing on, nothing on
my phone woke up and I let it ring
the lights came back and I turned them off
nothing on, nothing on

## Final post-chorus
nothing on but the moon
(nothing on) nothing on but the moon
and the only thing glowing is you
