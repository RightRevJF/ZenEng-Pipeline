A Wire Pulled Tight, draft 1

Verse 1
There's a chair in the waiting room, white under the light
where the clock on the wall forgets how to move
a second stretched thin like a wire pulled tight
nothing to reach for, nothing to prove

Pre-chorus 1
And I have sat inside that silence
long enough to know

Chorus 1
That every hour is a wire
strung from the first cry to the last
it hums when no one's listening
it holds when you hold fast
I have felt every one
the ones that dragged and the ones that flew
and somewhere in the stillness
time is breaking through

Verse 2
There's a laugh at a table on a warm afternoon
where the hours slip by like water through hands
you look up and the light's gone halfway round the room
and nobody noticed and nobody planned

Pre-chorus 2
And I have drifted in that lightness
far enough to know

Chorus 2
(as chorus 1, with "I have lived every one")

Verse 3
There's a roar that hits before you know that it's real
sixty thousand strangers and your feet leave the ground
your hands find hands before your heart can feel
and for one held breath the clock makes no sound

Pre-chorus 3
And I have stood inside that roaring
close enough to know

Chorus 3
(as chorus 1, with "I have carried every one")

Verse 4
Then in a single second something gave way
the way a fever breaks at three a.m.
the heavy minutes stayed but the weight couldn't stay
and every wasted hour breathed again

Bridge
The light came in all at once
and I put the clock down
every wall I'd built against the waiting
came apart and let me out
The clock still ticking in the dark
I can hear it and not count
none of it was ever lost
and none of it was wasted

Final chorus
(as chorus 1, with "I have carried every one")

Tag
There's a chair in the waiting room, white under the light
and the weight inside it is a grace
