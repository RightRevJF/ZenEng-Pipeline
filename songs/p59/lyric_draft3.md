# The World Is Asking (draft 3)

Words and music: Jason Bryant

Verse 1
There's a door in my father's house
Painted shut before I was born
We hung our coats across the seam
And never asked what it was for
I grew up trusting what I could touch
The nail, the bread, the roof of tin
And if I heard a knock at night
I told myself it was the wind

Chorus
But the world is asking
In the hum beneath the small
In the stutter of my heart
In the first light of it all
It's the knock behind the wall
I was taught to call the wind
The world is asking
Will I let the question in

Verse 2
Three strangers working far apart
Who never knew each other's names
One counted beats inside a heart
One read the oldest light that came
One listened to the smallest hum
Where nothing ought to make a sound
And all of them found, every one
The same small circle coming round

Chorus

Bridge
I don't have proof, I have a door
I'm leaving open just a crack
If you're the one who's asking
I won't send the question back
Here I am
Speak, I'm listening

Last Chorus
And the world is asking
In the hum beneath the small
In the stutter of my heart
In the first light of it all
It's the knock behind the wall
And I won't call it the wind
Here I am, here I am
Speak, I'm listening
