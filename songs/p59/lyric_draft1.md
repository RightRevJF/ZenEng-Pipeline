# The World Is Asking (draft 1)

Words and music: Jason Bryant

Verse 1
They told me young the door was sealed
That what we see is all we get
The sky is only what it seems
And no one's ever answered yet
So I kept my questions in a drawer
I learned to call the quiet wise
I measured everything I saw
And never asked the reason why

Chorus
But the world is asking
In the hum beneath the small
In the heartbeat of a sparrow
In the first light of it all
The world is asking
And it cannot tell me why
It's been asking all along
Waiting on a reply

Verse 2
Three strangers working far apart
Who never knew each other's names
One counted beats inside a heart
One read the oldest light that came
One listened to the smallest hum
Where nothing ought to make a sound
And all of them found, every one
The same small circle coming round

Chorus

Bridge
I don't have proof, I have a door
I'm leaving open just a crack
If you're the one who's asking
I won't send the question back
Here I am
Speak, I'm listening

Last Chorus
And the world is asking
In the hum beneath the small
In the heartbeat of a sparrow
In the first light of it all
The world is asking
And I answer it tonight
Here I am, here I am
Speak, I'm listening
