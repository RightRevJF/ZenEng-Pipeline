# Strangers Till the Chorus (song 70), draft 4

## Verse 1
Bought two tickets back in May
hers is still inside my phone
wore the shirt from their first tour
fits me if I hold my breath
standing where we said we'd stand
left side of the mixing desk
texting no one so it looks
like somebody's on their way

## Pre-chorus 1
then a roadie taps the mic
and we cheer him like a god
four notes in, the screaming starts
and the whole room knows what's next

## Chorus 1
We were strangers till the chorus
with your voice inside my spine
a thousand mouths around one word
and I'll hum it in my sleep
we were strangers till the chorus
now I'd know you anywhere

## Verse 2
some guy in a work shirt yells
every word into my face
throws his arm around my neck
I don't even know his name
someone's singing at my back
humming in my shoulder blades
and I've put away the phone
needed both hands anyway

## Pre-chorus 2
now the floorboards start to give
and the bouncer knows the words
half of us are crying now
and we haven't hit it yet

## Chorus 2

## Bridge

and the singer steps away

you can hear the bar fridge hum
then the voice behind me starts
and we sing it on our own
and the singer turns around
just to hear us bring it down
