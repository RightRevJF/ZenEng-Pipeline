# Blue Movie — draft 1
Words and music: Jason Bryant

**Verse 1**
We slipped in masked beneath the sign
you read the rules and laughed at each
you snapped a picture, said it's mine
like you'd need proof of every breach

**Pre-chorus**
You pulled my mask down, broke the first
your mouth a red pen through the rest

**Chorus**
Let them have the screen, we've got the seats
let them fake it, we don't have to
blue movie, blue movie
starring nobody but you

**Post-chorus**
Blue, blue, blue movie
you and me in the blue movie

**Verse 2**
Black pleather sweating, cold to heat
you climb me while the speakers moan
the blue light licks you, beat by beat
the blonde up there is on her own

**Pre-chorus**

you laugh against my neck and climb

**Chorus**
Let them have the screen, we've got the seats
let them fake it, we don't have to
blue movie, blue movie
starring nobody but you

**Post-chorus**
Blue, blue, blue movie

**Bridge**
Don't let the house lights come up yet
don't let the credits roll
I'll know this like a tongue knows
the place a tooth has been

**Last chorus**
Let them have the screen, we've got the seats
let them fake it, we don't have to
blue movie, blue movie
starring nobody but you

**Last post-chorus**
The film still loops, there isn't any screen
