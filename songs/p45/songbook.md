# At a glance

**What it's about.** Two ordinary people slip into an adult cinema, masks on, heads down, paying cash. She reads the posted rules out loud, laughs at every one, and takes a picture of the sign. Then the two of them break the rules, one at a time, in the back rows, while the film on the screen plays to nobody. The song stays in that room and in the present tense: it is funny, it is hot, and they are enjoying every minute. Only the very last lines step outside. The film still loops, but there isn't any screen: the night keeps playing in his head after it has ended.

**How it goes.** Up-tempo dance-pop, ♩ = 118, four on the floor, straight sixteenths, in A minor. The hook is a little falling figure on "blue movie": the plucked synth plays it in the intro before anyone sings, the singer sings it in the chorus, and the post-chorus chants it. The drums drop out for the bridge and everything comes back for the last chorus. The last post-chorus is the quiet one.

# Section by section

Pitches are where the voice sounds. Bar numbers count from the first bar of the intro.

## Intro (bars 1–8)

The plucked synth plays the "blue movie" figure alone for four bars over the chord changes. Pad and bass come in at bar 5 with the kick on every beat and open hats on the off-beats.

## Verse 1 (bars 9–16)

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| Am F | We paid cash, kept our heads down past the sign |
| C G | you read the rules out loud and laughed at each |
| Am F | you snapped a picture, said it's mine |
| C G | like you'd need proof of every breach |

The first line comes in on the "and" of beat 2 in bar 9 and tumbles down from D4 to D3, the lowest note in the song, on the "and" of beat 4, then climbs back through G3 and A3 to E4 and C4. The second line is the same tune. Lines 3 and 4 sit higher, around C4 to E4, and move fast: quick sixteenth-note syllables on "every breach." This verse is dense on purpose. Keep it crisp and rhythmic, almost spoken.

## Pre-chorus 1 (bars 17–20)

| Chords | Line |

Both lines start on G3 and jump to D4, C4, E4, then settle back to G3. "First" is held for two beats. The claps come in here, with a snare build through bar 20.

## Chorus 1 (bars 21–28)

"Starring nobody but you" lifts straight back to G4 on "no-bo-" (bar 27) and comes down to B3 on "you."

Everything pulls back to pad and held bass, and the voice gets softer. The first line repeats C4 and jumps to F4 in the middle of "isn't," staying there through "any screen." # For each player

**Voice.** Light tenor, D3 to G4. Sing the verses close and rhythmic, nearly spoken. Lean into the chorus: the hook "blue movie" wants a clean, bright G4 and an even fall. The post-chorus is a chant, so think group vocal. The last post-chorus is quiet: the night is over and it is still playing.

**Drums.**

- *Intro:* tacet bars 1–4; kick on every beat and open hats on the off-beats from bar 5.
- *Verses:* kick and off-beat open hats; claps on 2 and 4 in verse 2 only.
- *Pre-choruses:* claps on 2 and 4; a snare build through the last bar.
- *Choruses:* crash on 1, claps, off-beat sixteenth closed hats over the open hats.
- *Post-choruses:* kick, open hats, claps.
- *Bridge:* out for bars 57–62; a snare build in bars 63–64, eighths then sixteenths.
- *Last chorus:* everything, with a crash on 1 and at bar 69.
- *Last post-chorus:* out, except the kick in bars 75–76.
- *Outro:* kick and hats; one crash with the last chord.

**Synth bass.** Eighth notes, root and octave alternating, from bar 5. Whole notes in the bridge (bars 57–62) and held notes in the last post-chorus. One low A to end. Range A1 to G3.

**Pluck (the figure).** The "blue movie" figure an octave above the voice (D5 to G5): the intro (bars 1–8), each post-chorus, the whole last chorus, the outro. Short and bright.

**Pad.** A held chord each bar from bar 5, with a slow flicker (a gate or tremolo) like a projector. An octave higher in the choruses, a little louder in the bridge. One A minor chord to end. Range A3 to C5.

# Arrangement

The figure comes before the words: the listener hears "blue movie" on the pluck in bar 1 and recognises it when the singer arrives at it in bar 25. The verses are lean, the choruses full, the post-choruses a chant. The bridge takes the drums away so that the tongue-and-tooth image lands in a quiet room, then the build brings everything back for the last chorus, the loudest moment. The last post-chorus strips back down again: the only place the song lets the longing show.

## Producer one-sheet

**The emotional target.** Two ordinary people being very naughty and loving every minute: cheeky, physical, fun. Underneath, very faintly, the knowledge that a night like this only plays once. Keep the heat in front and let the longing in only at the end.

**The three references.**

- **Dua Lipa, "Houdini"** (2023), the lead: 117 BPM, D minor, sexy and mysterious disco-pop. Take the tempo, the minor colour and the confident, slightly arch vocal.
- **Troye Sivan, "Rush"** (2023): house-pop at 126 BPM, openly physical. Take the shamelessness and the chant energy for the post-chorus.
- **Ariana Grande, "yes, and?"** (2024): four-on-the-floor house at 119 BPM under a sultry vocal. Take the kick-and-clap pocket and the vocal close up front.

**The dynamic arc.**

| Section | Bars | Level | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | 1–8 | mp → mf | the figure alone, then kick, hats, bass, pad |
| Verses | 9–16, 33–40 | mf | lean: kick, hats, bass (claps in verse 2) |
| Pre-choruses | 17–20, 41–44 | mf ↗ | claps; the snare build |
| Choruses, post-choruses | 21–32, 45–56 | f | full; the chant |
| Bridge | 57–64 | mp ↗ | drums out, then the build |
| Last chorus | 65–72 | ff | everything, the figure under it |
| Last post-chorus | 73–76 | p | pad and held bass; the voice softer |
| Outro | 77–80 | mf | the figure; one chord, let ring |

**The non-negotiables.** The figure in the intro before the voice. "Blue movie" falling cleanly, the second time a step lower, and "starring nobody but you" lifting back to the top. The post-chorus as a chant. The drums out for the bridge. The last post-chorus quiet. The lyric stays as written: its mix of humour, heat and absence is the point.

**The mix priorities.** The voice on top and close, every word clear, including the jokes. The kick and the bass lock together; the pluck sits bright above the pad. In the last post-chorus, the voice and the pad only.

## Production brief

*The song.* An up-tempo dance-pop song about two people in an adult cinema, breaking every rule on the sign: playful, explicit in feeling but not in words, with one quiet turn at the end.

*References* (checked by search):

- **Dua Lipa, "Houdini."** 117 BPM in D minor; reviewers heard it as sexy, mysterious and melodramatic.
- **Troye Sivan, "Rush."** House-pop at 126 BPM; a hypnotic, openly sensual anthem.
- **Ariana Grande, "yes, and?"** A four-on-the-floor house track at 119 BPM, with Madonna's "Vogue" behind it.

*Non-negotiables.* As on the one-sheet.

## Session notes

- **Tempo and click.** ♩ = 118, 4/4, straight sixteenths. Click on.
- **Key.** A minor. The E chord in the bridge (bars 63–64) brings a G♯.
- **Chords per bar.** One chord a bar, as in the form table and the chart.
- **Range.** Voice D3–G4. Synth bass A1–G3. Pluck D5–G5. Pad A3–C5.
- **Tracking.** Drums and bass first, then the pad and the pluck, then the voice. Double the post-chorus chant (two or three voices).
- **Blend and panning.** Voice up the middle and close. Kick and bass centred. Pad wide left and right; pluck slightly right. Claps centred, hats slightly left.
