## Production brief

**The song.** Driving rock about a paradox: things stand because they're pushed from both sides. An arch, a man on a wire, two rafters at a roof's ridge, and finally the singer's own body. The sound should carry the idea, full of tension that never quite settles. The band plays every chord change an eighth early. The verses are coiled and muted, and the choruses let go. It should sound physical and dry, not epic or polished.

**References**

- **Queens of the Stone Age, "No One Knows" (2002):** a riff built from simple power chords that pushes against the drums. Use it as the model for the riff guitar and the push.
- **Foo Fighters, "The Pretender" (2007):** dynamics that shift from section to section, tight verses exploding into the chorus, and a drop before the last big push. Use it as the model for the verse-to-chorus release and the stripped chorus 3.
- **Royal Blood, "Figure It Out" (2014):** about three minutes, riff-driven and tense, with the groove locked between the bass and the drums. Use it as the model for the length, the low end and the tightness.

**Non-negotiables**

1. ♩ = 104 from start to finish, played tight to the click.
2. **The push.** The bass and rhythm guitar play every new chord on the "and" of 4 and tie it over the bar line. It's on the charts as an accent.
3. The verses stay muted and tight; the choruses open up. Don't let the verses get loud.
4. **Chorus 3 drops out:** clean guitar, soft organ and voice only, then a snare build in its last bar.
5. **The peak is "stand" in bar 68** (A4 over D, with a crash and china), then "holds" on G4 in bar 69. Nothing earlier is bigger.
6. The song ends on one E minor hit, left to ring. It doesn't resolve to G.
7. Keep the voice dry and in front. Every word has to be heard.

## Session notes

**Tempo and metre.** 78 bars, about 3:00. 4/4 at ♩ = 104 throughout; click in eighths if the push drifts.

**Key map**

| Bars | Section | Key | Chords (per bar) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–8 | Intro | E minor | Em Em C D, Em Em C D |
| 9–16 / 29–36 | Verse 1 / 2 | E minor | Em Em C D, Em Em C D |
| 17–24 / 37–44 / 53–60 | Chorus 1 / 2 / 3 | G major | G D Em C, G D C G |
| 25–28 | Riff | E minor | Em Em C D |
| 45–52 | Bridge | G major | C C D D Am Am D D |
| 61–70 | Chorus 4 | G major | G D Em C, G D, C D G G |
| 71–78 | Outro | E minor | Em Em C D, Em Em C Em |

One key signature throughout. The D that ends each verse line is the V chord of G and pulls into the chorus. The bridge holds D (bars 51–52) to keep the pressure on before chorus 3.

**Range**

- **Voice:** E3–A4.
- **Verses:** E3–C4.
- **Choruses:** up to G4, reached only on "holds" and "stand."
- **Bridge:** up to F♯4 on "stand."
- **A4:** once, on the last "stand" (bar 68).

**Tracking**

1. Track drums and bass to the click first, then the rhythm guitar, locking the push (the next chord on the "and" of 4) between all three.
2. Track the riff guitar for the intro, bars 25–28 and the outro, as written.
3. Track the lead vocal with the full band in the headphones. Sing chorus 3 close and quiet, then give chorus 4 everything.
4. For chorus 3, switch the rhythm guitar to a clean sound and let one chord ring per bar.
5. The organ plays from the chord symbols:
   - one chord a bar;
   - low in the verses, up in the choruses, rising through the bridge, soft in chorus 3;
   - out in the intro, riff and outro until the final hit.

**Blend and panning**

- Vocal centre, dry and in front.
- Rhythm guitar to one side, the riff guitar to the other.
- Organ slightly off-centre.
- Bass and kick centre.
- The verses should sound narrow and close, the choruses wide.

## Parts pack

The parts PDF has seven pages. Every part is written out bar by bar and matches the demo note for note, with chord symbols, section names and key changes. Bar numbers sit at the start of each line. Muted notes are marked staccato, and pushed notes are accented.

- **Riff guitar (p. 1):** every note of the intro, bars 25–28 and the outro. Sounds an octave below the written notes.
- **Rhythm guitar (pp. 2–3):** every chord. Sounds an octave below the written notes.
  - Verses: palm-muted.
  - Choruses: open eighths.
  - Bridge: the build.
  - Chorus 3: clean, let ring.
- **Bass (pp. 4–5):** every note. Sounds an octave below the written notes.
  - Verses: muted.
  - Choruses, riff and outro: eighths.
  - Bridge: quarters, then eighths.
  - Chorus 3: tacet.
- **Drums (pp. 6–7):** every hit on a drum staff, including:
  - the bridge build;
  - the snare build in bar 60;
  - the crash and china in bar 68;
  - the tom fill in bar 70.

There's no written keys part; the organ plays from the chord symbols, as the session notes describe.
