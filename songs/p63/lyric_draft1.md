Draft 1 (Common Meter, 8.6.8.6; narrator)

Verse 1
Before the hills, before the sea
before the first of days
there was a Song, and at its sound
the stars began to blaze

Refrain
Lullay, lullay, the Song is small
lullay, the stars are near
the One who sang the world awake
is sleeping, sleeping here

Verse 2
That Song came down one winter night
as small as songs can be
a cry, a breath, a hungry voice
on Mary's tired knee

Verse 3
She hummed the tune her mother hummed
and rocked him in the hay
and he who taught the stars to sing
was sung to sleep that way

Verse 4
And he will sing again one day
when all the night is done
and over us, with joy, the Song
will sing us, every one
