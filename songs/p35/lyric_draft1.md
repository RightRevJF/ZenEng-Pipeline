# The First Wood (draft 1)

1
I know this wood: a feed-trough, pine,
cut green and roughly squared;
the adze ran crooked down the line,
the edges never pared.
  Sleep in the first wood, child,
  and I will hold you too.

2
These hands have joined a hundred beds
for other fathers' sons;
tonight I run my thumb along
to find the splintered ones.
  Sleep in the first wood, child,
  and I will hold you too.

3
I'll teach you how to read the grain,
to set a mortise true;
your hands will learn the saw, the plane,
the work my father knew.
  Sleep in the first wood, child,
  and I will hold you too.

4
I know what weight a beam will bear,
how long a post will stand;
I cannot plane the wood out there
that waits beyond my hand.
  Sleep in the first wood, child;
  it will not be the last.
