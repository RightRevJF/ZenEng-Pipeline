# The Word Without a Word — draft 3

**Verse 1**
The voice that said, Let there be light,
and light came running at the call,
is lying in a feeding trough
with not a single word at all.

**Refrain**
The Word without a word
is sleeping on her chest;
the breath that made the world
goes in and out, and rests.

**Verse 2**
She'll soften bread and say it, bread,
and set a crumb against his tongue;
she'll cup his hand to catch the rain,
then lift him up where stars are hung.

**Refrain**

**Interlude** (her lullaby): the cello hums the refrain's tune at half voice; in the last two bars the harmony lifts toward the bridge.

**Verse 3**
The tongue that thundered on the mount
is milk and spit and half a sound;
he loses it and tries again,
and she says *ma* until it's found.

**Bridge**
And when he gets a whole word out,
it's not a word he had to learn;
he reaches up past both of them.
Now: *Abba*. And Joseph turns.

**Verse 4 (a step higher)**
He cries, red-faced, and shakes with it,
a thin cry, hoarse against her ear;
now all that God has left to say
is crying, and his mother hears.

**Final refrain (broadened)**
The Word without a word
is what God meant to say:
the breath that made the world
goes in, and out, and in.

*Anchors for the arranger:* "ma" (verse 3) and "Abba" (bridge) are the song's structural words. Each gets its own held note, the accompaniment thins under it, and nothing else moves.
