# The Word Without a Word — draft 1

**Verse 1**
The voice that said, Let there be light,
and light came running at the call,
is lying in a feeding trough
with not a single word at all.

**Refrain**
O Word without a word,
the Speech that cannot speak:
the breath that made the world
is warm against her cheek.

**Verse 2**
She'll teach him bread, she'll teach him rain,
she'll point and say the word for star;
the things he named before the world
he'll learn from her for what they are.

**Refrain**

**Interlude**

**Verse 3**
The tongue that thundered on the mount
will stumble over every sound;
the God who wrote the law in stone
will learn his letters on the ground.

**Bridge**
And when he speaks his first true word,
it will not be a word that's new:
the name he called before the worlds,
now Abba, small and human too.

**Verse 4 (a step higher)**
God spoke through prophets, fire and cloud,
in many ways, in many years;
now all that God has left to say
is crying, and his mother hears.

**Final refrain (broadened)**
O Word without a word,
when all is said and done,
the breath that made the world
is breathing in the Son.
