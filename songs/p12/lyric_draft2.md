# Wood and Cloth: second edition, lyric draft 2 (21 Nov 2026)

1
Wood that fed the oxen held him
Maker small enough to tend
wood would hold him, raised for dying
wood and cloth at either end

2
Mary wrapped him close in linen
warm against the winter night
Joseph wrapped him close in linen
cold behind a stone from sight

3
Myrrh the wise men laid before him
spice they keep to wrap the dead
pounds of myrrh his friends would carry
for a borrowed garden bed

4
Strangers called him king of Judah
gold beneath a star laid down
Pilate wrote the same above him
gave him thorn to be his crown

5
Linen lies where God was lying
stone away and none to find
once the manger held him sleeping
wood and cloth are left behind
