# The Song You Sang Me - draft 1
V1: You're folding the same napkin by the window / you ask me if I've come to fix the heat / I pull my chair in close like I've always done / It's me. I'm your son.
V2: I brought the pictures from the lake in August / you touch a face and say, "she used to swim" / you hold my hand the way you'd hold anyone / It's me. I'm your son.
V3: You don't ask me who I am now / you watch the birds come to the feeder / I tell you anyway, the way you'd leave a light on / It's me. I'm your son.
B (the lullaby): Lay your head, the lamp is low / the river knows the way to go / I'll leave the hall light on for you / and I'll be here the whole night through
V4: I sang it soft, and somewhere in the second line / your hand came up and kept the time / you didn't know my face, but you sang along / It's me. I'm your song.
