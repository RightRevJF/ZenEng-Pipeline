# Either Or (draft 5)

Verse 1
Aristotle drew a line across the dust
said one thing's true and the other must be lies
and we've bled across that line ever since
calling our own blood the other side

Refrain
and the wind blows where it wants to blow
it doesn't stop to ask you yes or no
you can't fence it in, you can't keep score
the wind was never either or

Verse 2
they sat in the tower systematizing wind
weighing the wind by the words that they heard
sprinkled holy water on the laws they wrote
and the herd said amen, word for word

Refrain / break

Verse 3
the ones who won't choose catch the stones
from the keepers of the dead
handing down their fathers' wars by name
turning bones into bread

Build / Refrain

Last refrain
and the wind blows where it wants to blow
it doesn't stop to ask you yes or no
