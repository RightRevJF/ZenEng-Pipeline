# Never Keep Perfect Time (song 71), draft 1

## Verse 1
Before the wind had learned to blow
before the sea could turn
the dark lay folded four ways round
with nothing there to burn
and where the corners met and pressed
a small thing woke and beat
it caught, and lost, and caught again
and missed, and found its feet

## Chorus 1
Never keep perfect time
the old dark told the heart
a clock's a dead thing's rhyme
the stumble is your art
never keep perfect time

## Verse 2
It went into the wren and whale
the hare beneath the snow
and every chest that rose and fell
ran fast, and then ran slow
the tide picked up the broken step
the moon took up the song
and every living thing that sings
has learned to sing it wrong

## Chorus 2

## Verse 3
Had it been folded three ways round
we'd beat another tune
so lay your ear against a chest
and hear the folded room
the four dark corners of the world
are drumming in the bone
and every time it misses once
you know you are not stone

## Bridge
And when a heart runs like a mill
and every beat's the same
the old ones know the dark has come
to call it back by name
so sing it ragged, sing it wild
and never sing it clean
the world is in the falling short
and in the space between
