# Never Keep Perfect Time (song 71), draft 2

## Verse 1
Before the wind had learned to blow
before the sea could turn
the dark lay folded four ways round
with nothing there to burn
and where the corners met and pressed
a small thing woke and beat
it caught, and lost, and caught again
and missed, and found its feet

## Chorus 1
Never keep perfect time
skip like a stone on the sea
a clock's a dead thing's rhyme
the dark keeps a drum in me
never keep perfect time

## Verse 2
It slipped into a fiddle bow
the hitch before the reel
it went into the drunkard's feet
the long way round the field
it caught inside a widow's breath
that snags upon a name
the mill wheel tried to keep it straight
and never turned the same

## Verse 3
Had it been folded three ways round
we'd keep another time
so lay your ear against a chest
and hear the dark inside
the four old corners of the world
are drumming in the bone
and where it skips, a sparrow's width
the whole night rushes home

## Bridge
And when a heart runs like a mill
and every beat's the same
the old ones go and stop the clocks
and cover up the glass
so sing it ragged, sing it wild
and let it fall and catch
the cracked bell carries farthest out
across the dark, and back
