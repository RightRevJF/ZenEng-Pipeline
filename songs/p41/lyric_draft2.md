# The Water Used to Sing — draft 2
*Words and music: Jason Bryant*

**Verse 1**
It came off the ridge in a hurry,
loud as a county fair,
took the low field every April,
left it black by June;
we cursed it over supper
and swam in it after dark.

**Pre-chorus 1**
I was young and I knew better,
and I knew what to do with stone:

**Chorus**
The water used to sing
the whole night under my window,
the water used to sing.
Now it only shines.

**Verse 2**
I poured the wall in August,
forty feet of patient grey,
and the river lay down like a sheepdog
when it hears its name;
they cut a ribbon, shook my hand,
the lights came on in town.

**Pre-chorus 2**
And the lake lay flat as a table,
not a ripple, not a sound:

**Chorus**

**Bridge**
Below the wall the willows went to paper,
the corn came up in matchsticks,
the mill wheel stopped like a dead man's watch,
the kids forgot the swimming hole,
and my girl said, "Daddy, what's a river sound like?"
I didn't have an answer.

**Break** (instrumental: the gate)

**Verse 3**
I climbed the wall in the dark and turned the wheel,
it fought me like a mule,
then the whole lake leaned against the gate
and the gate let go;
it went down the valley shouting,
took my fence and the plaque with my name.

**Last chorus**
The water used to sing
the whole night under my window,
the water's singing now,
loud and crooked in the dark.
It took the fence, it took the field,
it will take whatever I build;
living water doesn't shine.
It sings. (held)
