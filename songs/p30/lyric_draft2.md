# She Left the Light On — draft 2
Words and music: Jason Bryant

Intro: the guitar lick (4 bars)

**Verse 1**
Seventeen and an hour late, I'd cut the engine at the road
Coast the last of the gravel in like Mama wouldn't know
Never wondered who'd been oiling that screen door all along
And every time I topped that hill, she left the light on

**Chorus 1**
She left the light on
It never asked me where I'd been
It just showed me the way back in
She left the light on

Post-chorus: the guitar lick (2 bars)

**Verse 2**
When my own boy got the keys and the clock went past two
I stood at the window doing what she used to do
That was the year her knees gave out; I hooked her porch light to my phone
And lit hers too at sundown, three hundred miles from home

**Chorus 2**
She left the light on
It never asked me where I'd been
It just showed me the way back in
She left the light on

Post-chorus: the guitar lick (2 bars)

**Bridge**
There's a button on my phone that still says Mama's Porch
Every evening right at dark, it comes on by itself
I've had a year to shut it off; it'd only take my thumb

**Chorus 3 (the flip)**
Now you're the one out late
So I leave the light on
I won't ask you where you've been
I'll just show you the way back in
I leave the light on

Outro: the guitar lick, ending unresolved
