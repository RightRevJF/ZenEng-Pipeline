## Findings

1. **No sixteenth grid.** The engine's metres all count eighths; a funk-pop vocal moves in sixteenths. **The run's port:** a metre "4/4s" (16 slots a bar; stresses on the eighths; quarter beats strong; line-final stress on a quarter); the walk's steps in sixteenths, longer for sparse lines; durations, the 16th-note engraving split, MIDI ticks and the calibration's quarter conversion all read the slot. **Proposed fix 37:** the slot (eighth or sixteenth) becomes a field of the metre, and every rule that counts slots reads it.
2. **The breath rule counts notes, not time.** "At most 13 notes without a rest" killed nearly every build: a verse line in sixteenths carries 13–19 syllables in 4.3 seconds. **The run's port:** a breath is an eighth's rest; no run longer than 4.6 seconds; every line ends an eighth before the next. **Proposed fix 38:** state the breath rule in seconds.
3. **Numbers and line-final words read as function words.** The lexicon reads "one" in "five-oh-ONE", and "now", "here" and "mine" at a line's end, as unstressed. The hook died in the room on its weak ending; the second run's pick left "MINE" unstressed. **The run's port:** "one" after "oh" is a content word; a line-final adverb of time or place, or a possessive pronoun standing alone, carries the line's last stress. **Proposed fix 39:** add both to v58's stress rules, beside fixes 7, 19 and 24.
4. **The survival band reads nothing in half the song.** 94.8% survived (v65 fix 28's band is 5–70%). The title cell fixes most of the chorus, the hooks are designed, and the calibration reads only verse 1 and chorus 1: the pre-choruses, the breakdown and the lift face nothing but the floors. **Proposed fix 40:** read the calibration over every sung section that isn't designed, and drop designed lines from the band.
5. **9.12 saturates on a sixteenth groove.** The scratch guitar plays every sixteenth, so the event count is at its ceiling in every section with the band in; the arc compressed to 0.54–0.92, and the last choruses read level with the first until every part played harder. **Proposed fix 41:** count events in eighth windows, read velocity without ghost notes, and credit a key change as a lift.
6. **Short lines in long spans.** The breakdown's lines (8–10 syllables in 32 slots) rarely reached their CLAP on the walk's normal steps. Covered by fix 37's density-aware steps.
7. **New answers aren't checked against earlier ones.** "A party song" contradicted his own never-say list; the run caught it. **Proposed fix 42:** intake checks each answer against the lists already chosen and asks before going on.
8. **No role for a gang or a band's answer.** The lift's shout, HEY! and GO!, and the gang on the title cell had nowhere to live. **The run's port:** a "shout" lyric role, a gang singer and answer marks on the lead sheet. **Proposed fix 43:** a gang part (singer, role, its own demo track and staff marks), and band answers as cues.
9. **v65 fixes used as ports:** 9 and 24 (stress), 13 and 31 (form lengths), 14 (tenor row), 21 (quote kind), 26 (ASCII track names), 29 (line-final stress on a beat), 30 (aimed notes exempt), 32 (fitted cells clear the pickup), 33 (MIDI tempo in quarters), 34 (spelling from the key), 36 (production notes in the songbook).
