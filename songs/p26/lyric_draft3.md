# Five-Oh-One (draft 3)

**Verse 1**
five long days they've been borrowing every hour I've got
I've been watching that second hand like it owes me money
coat on the chair and my keys in my fist and one foot in the hall
then the minute rolls over and the whole place lets out its breath

**Pre-chorus**
somebody's loading the speakers in the back of a car
somebody's sending the address with a row of little flames

**Chorus**
five-oh-one, the whole town loosens its tie
five-oh-one, every light on the avenue's green
sixty hours and every single one of them's mine
five-oh-one, good luck finding me now

**Hook**
five-oh, five-oh, five-oh-one (hey!)
five-oh, five-oh, five-oh-one (go!)

**Verse 2**
radio's playing the song I pretended I hated in June
now I'm singing every word at a red light, and so is the guy in the truck
two blocks out and the bass is already rattling the glass
somebody's dancing in the driveway before I've even parked

**Pre-chorus**
the neighbor who swore he wasn't coming is dancing with a plate
somebody's grandmother's out in the middle, and she's winning

**Chorus · Hook**

**Breakdown** (claps and bass)
if your Monday tried to bury you, clap
if your Tuesday held the shovel, clap
if your Wednesday ran a week long, clap
if your Thursday wouldn't end, clap

**Lift**
look at your wrist and tell me, what does it say?
(everybody) FIVE-OH-ONE!

**Chorus** (up a step, in F)

**Last chorus**
five-oh-one, the whole town loosens its tie
five-oh-one, every light on the avenue's green
sixty hours and every single one of them's mine
five-oh-one, and you'll find me here

**Hook · Outro**
five-oh, five-oh, five-oh-one
