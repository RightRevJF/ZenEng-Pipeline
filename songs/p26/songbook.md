## What the song is about

Five long days somebody else had his hours. He's been watching the second hand like it owes him money. Then the minute rolls over, and the whole place lets out its breath.

Somebody's loading the speakers into a car. The address arrives with a row of little flames. By verse 2 he's singing at a red light with the guy in the next truck, the bass is rattling the glass two blocks out, and somebody's dancing in the driveway before he's even parked. The neighbor who swore he wasn't coming is dancing with a plate. Somebody's grandmother is out in the middle, and she's winning.

The chorus is the moment the week lets go: "five-oh-one, the whole town loosens its tie." Every chorus ends with a dare: "good luck finding me now." The last one answers it: "and you'll find me here." He's arrived.

It's a party song that never says the word. The people make the party.

**Playing it:**

- **Lock the pocket.** Straight sixteenths, nothing swung. The groove is the song's heart; keep it tight and let the voice ride on top.
- **The hook is for the crowd.** The chant, the HEY! and GO!, the breakdown's claps and the lift's shout are all built to be shouted back.
- **Save the lift.** The key change at bar 65 is the peak. Everything before it should leave room.

## The song, section by section

Each lyric line takes two bars; the chords of those bars are shown beside it. Most lines start with a quick pickup in the bar before.

### Intro · bars 1–4

*E♭ A♭ E♭ A♭.* The guitar's sixteenth-note scratch and the horns playing the chant (FIVE-oh FIVE-oh FIVE-oh-ONE) alone for two bars. Drums and bass come in at bar 3.

### Verse 1 · bars 5–12

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| E♭ · A♭ | five long days they've been borrowing every hour I've got |
| E♭ · A♭ | I've been watching that second hand like it owes me money |
| E♭ · A♭ | coat on the chair and my keys in my fist and one foot in the hall |
| Cm · B♭ | then the minute rolls over and the whole place lets out its breath |

- **Melody:** low and talky, F3–E♭4, packed with sixteenths. "Borrowing" touches E♭4 on the "and" of beat 3 in bar 5; "money" sits on C4; "breath" lands on B♭3 on beat 3 of bar 12 and holds.
- **Band:** drums in the pocket with eighth hats; bass on the root with an octave pop on the "and" of 2; guitar stabs on every "and".

### Pre-chorus 1 · bars 13–16

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| A♭ · B♭ | somebody's loading the speakers in the back of a car |
| Cm · B♭ | somebody's sending the address with a row of little flames |

- **Melody:** up a notch, B♭3–E♭4. "Car" lands on D4 at beat 2 of bar 14; "flames" holds on D4 from beat 3 of bar 16.
- **Band:** the electric piano comes in; the kick adds the "a" of 2; a snare-and-tom fill on beats 3–4 of bar 16.

### Chorus 1 · bars 17–24

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| E♭ · B♭ | five-oh-one, the whole town loosens its tie |
| Cm · A♭ | five-oh-one, every light on the avenue's green |
| E♭ · B♭ | sixty hours and every single one of them's mine |
| A♭ · B♭ | five-oh-one, good luck finding me now |

- **The title:** every "five-oh-one" is the same figure: a dotted eighth, a sixteenth and an eighth, climbing the E♭ chord B♭3–E♭4–G4, with ONE on beat 2. G4 is the top of the chorus.
- **Melody:** "tie" lands on E♭4 at beat 3 of bar 18; "green" on C4 at beat 3 of bar 20; "mine" on D4 at beat 3 of bar 22; "now" on D4 at beat 2 of bar 24, held.
- **Band:** full groove, sixteenth hats, crash on bar 17; horns stab on 1 and the "and" of 2.

### Hook 1 · bars 25–28

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| E♭ · A♭ | five-oh, five-oh, five-oh-one (band: HEY!) |
| E♭ · A♭ | five-oh, five-oh, five-oh-one (band: GO!) |

- **The chant:** a 3+3+2 tresillo: FIVE-oh, FIVE-oh, FIVE-oh-ONE, B♭3 and E♭4 three times, then ONE on G4 at the downbeat of the next bar. The horns play it with the voice.
- **The answers:** the band shouts HEY! (bar 26) and GO! (bar 28) on the "and" of beat 3.
- **Band:** claps on 2 and 4.

### Verse 2 · bars 29–36

### Lift · bars 61–64

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| A♭ · B♭ | look at your wrist and tell me, what does it say? |
| C · C | (everybody) FIVE-OH-ONE! |

- **Melody:** the question climbs from B♭3 to "say" on F4 at beat 2 of bar 62.
- **The shout:** everybody, on three hits in bar 63: FIVE (E4, beat 1) – OH (E4, beat 2) – ONE (G4, beat 3, held).
- **Band:** bars 61–62 hats and kick, the guitar rings; bars 63–64 over C, a sixteenth snare build. C is the door into F.

### Chorus 3 · bars 65–72

Up a whole step into F. The same chorus: the title figure now climbs C4–F4–A4, and the chorus tops out at A4.

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| F · C | five-oh-one, the whole town loosens its tie |
| Dm · B♭ | five-oh-one, every light on the avenue's green |
| F · C | sixty hours and every single one of them's mine |
| B♭ · C | five-oh-one, good luck finding me now |

- **Band:** everything harder: open hats on every "and", a crash every two bars, horns stab and hold the chord, and the gang shouts every "five-oh-one" with him.

### Chorus 4 · bars 73–80

As chorus 3, until the last line:

| Chords | Line |
|---|---|
| B♭ · C | five-oh-one, and you'll find me **here** |

- **The turn:** "here" is the song's top note: B♭4, on beat 4 of bar 79, over B♭, held across the C bar.

### Hook 3 · bars 81–84 · Outro · bars 85–88

The chant in F (HEY! and GO! again). The outro sings it once more, ONE held on A4. One hit on F at bar 87, then stop.

## For the players

- **Drums:** the groove is kick on 1, the "a" of 2 and 3; snare on 2 and 4; sixteenth hats with the open hat on the "and" of 4. Verses: kick on 1 and 3, eighth hats. Pre-choruses: eighth hats, with a sixteenth fill in the last bar. Breakdown: kick on 1 only. Lift bars 63–64: a sixteenth snare build to the downbeat of bar 65.
- **Bass (slap or fingers):** root on 1, root again on the "a" of 1, octave on the "and" of 2, root on 3, fifth and octave pops, a chromatic approach into the next bar. Verses lighter (root, octave, root, approach); the breakdown busiest.
- **Guitar:** the sixteenth chicken-scratch. Muted ghosts on every sixteenth, the chord's top three notes stabbed on each "and". Verse 1 keeps it to the stabs until bar 7. In the lift, let the chord ring.
- **Keys (electric piano):** hold the chord through each bar in the pre-choruses, choruses, hooks and lift. Lay out in the verses and the breakdown.
- **Horns (trumpet, sax, trombone):** the chant in the intro, the hooks and the outro, with the voice. Stabs on 1 and the "and" of 2 in every chorus; in choruses 3 and 4, also hold the chord on beats 3–4. The final hit on F.
- **Claps:** 2 and 4 in the hooks and the last choruses; three after every CLAP in the breakdown.
- **Gang (everyone who isn't singing lead):** HEY! and GO! after the hook lines; FIVE-OH-ONE in bar 63; every "five-oh-one" in choruses 3 and 4.

## For the singer

**Tenor: E♭3–A4, B♭4 once.**

- **The verses are close to speech.** Sit back on the sixteenths and let the consonants do the rhythm. Don't sing over the groove; sing inside it.
- **The title figure is the same every time:** short-short-long, ONE on beat 2. Hit it like a horn.
- **Breathe** in the eighth rest before each line; every line has one.
- **Verse 2's second line** (bar 30) is the tightest in the song. Practise it slowly.
- **After the lift,** everything is a step higher: the chorus tops out at A4 instead of G4.
- **The B♭4 on "here" (bar 79)** is the only time you go past A4. Land it on beat 4 and hold it through the bar.

## Production brief

**The song.** Friday at 5:01: five days somebody else had his hours, then the minute flips and he's gone, headed to a backyard full of people. A funk-pop party song for a tenor, a gang and a band with horns, in straight sixteenths at 112. It should feel like a band in a room having the best night of the week, with a crowd you can hear shouting back.

**References**

- **Mark Ronson featuring Bruno Mars, "Uptown Funk" (2014):** 4/4 at 115 BPM, with the Dap-Kings on horns. Its breakdown is four bars of vocals over a quarter-note kick, then a snare build ending in a stop. Take that drop-and-build for our breakdown and lift, and the horn section's hits.
- **Lizzo, "Juice" (2019):** a retro-inspired funk-pop song built on a throwback groove, with a tossed-off post-chorus. Take the post-chorus energy for our hook, and the lead's personality.
- **Bruno Mars, "24K Magic" (2016):** funk, disco and R&B, slower (94 BPM), with stacked background vocals from four singers. Take the gang of voices around the lead for our choruses 3 and 4.

**Non-negotiables**

1. **♩ = 112, straight sixteenths, no swing.** No tempo changes.
2. **The title figure never changes:** dotted eighth, sixteenth, eighth, ONE on beat 2.
3. **The breakdown drops the kit** (kick on 1 only), and the lift's snare build lands on the downbeat of bar 65.
4. **The key change is up a whole step at bar 65,** E♭ to F, through the C chord in bars 63–64.
5. **The B♭4 on "here" (bar 79) is the only note above A4.**
6. **Never name the party.** The lyric is locked; no ad-libs that name drinks, bars, clubs or the word "party".
7. **End on the hit.** One hit on F at bar 87, then silence. No fade.

**Range**

- **Voice:** tenor, E♭3–A4, with one B♭4 ("here", bar 79).
- **Verses:** E♭3–E♭4, low and talky.
- **Choruses:** B♭3–G4 in E♭; C4–A4 in F.
- **Gang:** HEY!, GO!, FIVE-OH-ONE; any comfortable octave.

**Tracking**

1. **Drums, bass and guitar together** to the sixteenth click. The pocket is everything: take the best groove over the cleanest take.
2. **Keys and horns next.** Track the horn chant tight to the guitar; the stabs short.
3. **Lead vocal.** Comp the verses for rhythm first, pitch second. Keep verse 2's second line light.
4. **Gang vocals last:** four to eight voices in a room, shouted, not sung pretty. Double them.
5. **Claps:** real hands, several people, slightly loose against the snare.

**Blend and panning**

- **Lead vocal:** centre and on top, dry, with a short slap.
- **Drums:** a tight kit, hats crisp; kick and bass sitting together.
- **Guitar:** left; **electric piano:** right; **horns:** right of centre, wide when they hold.
- **Gang and claps:** wide and roomy, so they feel like a crowd.
- **The lift:** the drop at bar 53 should feel like the room going quiet; the hit at bar 65 like the doors opening.
