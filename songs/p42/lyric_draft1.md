# Nothing Important — draft 1
*Words and music: Jason Bryant*

**Verse 1**
There's an old phone in the kitchen drawer,
one saved message, March the ninth;
you can hear his blinker ticking,
and he laughs before he says hello:

**Chorus**
Hey, it's me — nothing important,
I'm at the light on Seventh Street and thought of you;
hey, it's me, just wanted to hear you.
Call me back.

**Verse 2**
In October the battery swelled
and the screen came up off the case;
the kid at the counter said, "Nothing to save,"
I said, "There's one thing on it," and he looked away.

**Chorus**

**Bridge**
So I sat up with it one last night,
played it till the light went red,
the ticking and the laugh and Seventh Street,
and then it wouldn't turn back on.

**Verse 3**
Three days I kept the drawer shut,
didn't play the radio;
then the third morning at the sink I heard him,
the laugh before hello:

**Last Chorus**
Hey, it's me — nothing important,
I'm at the light on Seventh Street and thought of you;
hey, it's me, just wanted to hear you.
Hey, it's me.
Nothing important.
I'll call you back.
