# A Door in the Coral

Words and music: Jason Bryant (from his poem "Coral Shells")

Syllable plan: every verse line 7, so the three verses share one tune; the chorus five lines, 6 6 5 5 6, the hook at both ends; the last chorus turns 'I didn't know then' to 'and I know it now' and adds a line of 6; the bridge four lines of 6.

## Verse 1
Atlanta in the summer
holds the heat the way rooms do
after everyone has gone
and the cold air gives up too
I was nineteen I drove down
on two tallboys and a choice
on I-85 at night
Red Roof sign and highway noise

## Chorus 1
A door in the coral
it opened that summer
and never has closed
I didn't know then
a door in the coral

## Verse 2
The bedspread had coral shells
printed there before my time
cigarettes and beer gone warm
asphalt and gardenia
Her hair across the pillow
and my hands still learning how
the shells beneath us open
small pink mouths that wouldn't close

## Chorus 2
A door in the coral
it opened that summer
and never has closed
I didn't know then
a door in the coral

## Bridge
I have not recovered
not because it broke me
because it opened me
the heat is in me still

## Verse 3
Morning came in through the gap
red sign paling into gray
checkout at eleven sharp
I drove home the longer way
Years of other motel rooms
none of them have held the heat
still I look for coral shells
on the bed before I sleep

## Final chorus
A door in the coral
it opened that summer
and never has closed
and I know it now
I'm still standing in it
a door in the coral
