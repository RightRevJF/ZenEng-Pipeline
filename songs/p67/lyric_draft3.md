# Jehovah's Band (draft 3)
Words and music: Jason Bryant

## Verse 1
Down in the basement of the attic of my mind
A voice like gravel and honey came up the stair
It called me by a name that wasn't mine and I said yes
And I carried my spark like a match down there

## Chorus
I been sitting in with Jehovah's band
Out across the sands of time
The moon was keeping the beat for us
And the angels sang glory in rhyme
I been sitting in with Jehovah's band
Happy as a child and a long way from home

## Verse 2
My bare heels pounding the red dirt flat
Sweat in my eyes and the moon running down my back
Laughing I slipped through a seam to the world next door
We were dancing there and the beast couldn't follow that

## Bridge
Then a county man in a sweat-stained cap
Leaned on his truck where the meadow turned to sand
Said son, that sand ain't supposed to sing
And he laughed till the drummer laid down his hands
My spark went dead like a pinched-off wick
And I lost the sound of my daughter's name

## Verse 3
I came to in a booth with a cold cup of coffee
Empty as the sugar jar, didn't know the town
A waitress with hair black as coal called me honey
And the word just rolled off the table and down
Then home, and my daughter came laughing down the hall
Gold hair shining and she called me by my name

## Last Chorus

Not out across the sands of time

And her voice singing the angels' rhyme
I been sitting in with Jehovah's band
Thank God the band followed me home
Thank God the band followed me home
