# Across the Aisle — lyric draft 1
Words and music: Jason Bryant

[Verse 1]
Tuesday night, a thousand a plate,
Wednesday night, the other side;
same shrimp, same band, same donor's hand
on every shoulder, every time.
Morning in the hearing room,
they pound the desk and call it war;
the trade clears at nine-thirty-one:
they knew the vote the night before.

[Chorus]
Vote for me, I'll fix it all,
vote for me, I'll fix it all;
I'll shake his hand across the aisle,
we've got it fixed by fall.

[Verse 2]
The bill was written down the street
by a firm that pays them both;
back home the bridge is rusting through,
the ribbon's for a road they own.
They shut the government at dawn
and keep their paychecks through the fight;
the guard works on without his pay,
and both sides sleep just fine tonight.

[Chorus]

[Bridge]
They leave the Hill on the same day,
they take the same seat on the board;
one lake, two docks, one lobby firm,
both names in gold above the door.

[Guitar solo]

[Last chorus — the duet: two tracks of one singer]
(one)  Vote for me, I'll fix it all,
(two)  vote for me, I'll fix it all;
(both) I'll shake his hand across the aisle,
       we've got it fixed by fall.
(unison) Vote for us, we'll fix it all,
         vote for us, we'll fix it all;
         we'll shake hands across the aisle,
         we've got it fixed by fall.
         Across the aisle.
