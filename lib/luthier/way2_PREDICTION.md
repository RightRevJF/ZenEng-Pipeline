# Way 2 (the same order at every scale): predictions written before any scaling exponent was computed (18 Nov 2026)
Method: detrended fluctuation analysis (first order), scales from 4 to a quarter of the series, with a shuffled surrogate of each series as the control, as in the writer's bioacoustics work.
Series, per song: the pitch of each note; the time between onsets; and, on a sixteenth-note grid, whether a note begins.
S1. Kept songs' pitch series scale well above their shuffled surrogates (kept median alpha over 0.8; shuffled near 0.5).
S2. The engine's builds scale lower than kept songs in pitch (median at least 0.1 lower): they have less long-range order.
S3. Shuffling a kept song's bars lowers its pitch alpha in at least three songs in four. This is the damage the stage-7 gate cannot see.
S4. Among kept songs, nearness of alpha to 1 will NOT rank them by chart record beyond chance (|rho| under 0.15). I expect the exponent to tell real from made, not great from good.
S5. The fluctuation function of kept songs is straighter across scales than the builds' (smaller difference between the short-scale and long-scale slopes).
